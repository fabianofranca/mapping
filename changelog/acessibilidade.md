# Accessibility as a concept of the format — [Não lançado] (épico `epic/acessibilidade`, PR #1994, MCP 0.43.0, schema v20)

## Adicionado

- Specialization `formatVersion` 18: `accessibility` on the root (`touchTarget` `{ width, height, mode? }` in component units, `visual` by default or `expanded` when the platform grows the touch area to the minimum) and on the type (`role` from a closed list of WAI-ARIA names, `label` with the fields that give the accessible name, `touchTarget` or `false`, and `parts` for icons and items that come by field or by table row). In a `formatVersion` 17 or lower the keys are `only allowed with formatVersion 18`; the validation reports an unknown `role`, a `touchTarget` on a type without an interactive role, a `label` with a field that does not exist or is not `string` or `enum`, a part whose `when` is neither a field nor a table, a missing column, `fields` of another table, a repeated part `id` and a `touchTarget` on a part, at the exact path. `diffSpecs` does not count `accessibility` as a contract change.
- `accessibilityAudit` (`src/model/accessibility.ts`), a pure and linear audit: the pending item `accessible-label-missing` (a type or part with a required label and every label field empty, with the part and the table row) and the warning `touch-target-small` (a marking of an interactive type below the minimum converted by the container's density, with a 1 px tolerance; it is not a pending item and not counted in Incomplete). It audits the parts of a type with no role, skips the part of a field whose slot is full (`inactiveFields` of the drawing) and audits only the label of an annotation owned by an arrow.
- `docs/SPEC-FORMAT.md` ("Accessibility"), `docs/spec.schema.json`, `docs/AGENT-GUIDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, the `spec-author` skill and the `formatVersion` Help: the current version is 18.
- Test library `accessibility` (`tests/fixtures/accessibility`): a `button`, a bar with two field parts and a slot with a `fallback`, an `icon-button` and a type with a table part per row.
- Performance: the audit joins the derived-state budget of `tests/model/performance.test.ts` (8 ms per frame) and the growth tests of `npm run test:scale`.
- App (phase A2): the pending item `accessible-label-missing` appears in **Incomplete** and in the field (and in the cell of a table part) with the name of the field, of the part and the row (`Missing accessible label on the part Tab, row 2`); the warning `touch-target-small` appears in the new **Accessibility** section of the **Notices** window (only **Only visible layers** filters it) and as a line in the marking's **Details**, with the size in units and in pixels at the container's density; the status bar count adds the annotations with that warning, once per annotation, and **Incomplete** does not count it. The audit is a `computed` that pan and zoom do not touch (a test with a spy: 0 calls in 20 frames).
- MCP (phase A3, 0.43.0): `get_annotation`, each annotation of `get_marking` and the rows of `list_markings` carry `audit` (the `touch-target-small` findings with `actual`, `min`, `minPx` and `density`, in units and in pixels at the container's density) and `issues` with `accessible-label-missing` and the `part` (`id` and `name`); `get_project` counts the audit's items in the incomplete total; `get_component` and the type of `get_annotation` carry the resolved `accessibility`. `plan_changes` returns `touch-target-small` in `warnings` (with `marking` and `message`, only for the markings the batch creates or changes) and the audit's `accessible-label-missing` in `issues.new` and in the before, after and resolved counts. `get_code_hints` adds `accessibility` (`role`, `label`, `missing`, parts one per table row) only where the type declares it. The audit runs once per revision, memoized with the drawings.
- Test scripts: R9.40 (the pending item in the Details and `Ctrl+Z`), the new script R29 — Accessibility (block 3) and, in phase A3, R29.7 to R29.9 (reads, plan and `validate_specialization` through the MCP) and R12.44 (`touch-target-small` in `plan_changes`).
- Material 3 (Compose) library, version 17 (phase A4, `formatVersion` 18): root `accessibility.touchTarget` 48 × 48 `expanded`, which the ten types that Compose enlarges with `minimumInteractiveComponentSize` inherit (`button`, `extended-fab`, `fab`, `icon-button`, `icon-toggle-button`, `chip`, `checkbox`, `radio-button`, `switch` and `slider`), and `visual` on the `text-field`, `secure-text-field` and `search-bar`, whose field Compose does not enlarge. The closed list of roles and labels (`button`, `checkbox`, `radio`, `switch`, `slider`, `textbox`, `searchbox`, `dialog`, `image`, `progressbar` and `navigation`), the optional `<field>Description` fields with their `parts` on the icons of the `top-app-bar`, `bottom-app-bar`, `text-field`, `secure-text-field`, `search-bar`, `chip`, `switch`, `swipe-to-dismiss` and `alert-dialog`, and the `iconDescription` column with one part per row in the navigation bars, `tabs`, `dropdown-menu` and `segmented-button`. The version rises once, without a migration step (the change is additive); the drawing does not change. Two `top-app-bar` examples, "Labeled actions" and "Actions without a label", and the Fidelity of every touched type with the Compose lines and the two `roadmap` issues: #1998 (overlap of the expanded touch areas between neighbors) and #1999 (accessible role per `variants` case or field value).
- Test scripts (phase A4): R3.24 to R3.26 (`get_code_hints` with `accessibility` of the Icon button, the Top app bar part and the Slider), R17.58 (the accessibility fields do not change the drawing) and R29.10 and R29.11 (Material 3 in the app).
