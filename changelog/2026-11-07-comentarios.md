# Comments — 2026-11-07 (épico `epic/comentarios`, PR #724, MCP 0.25.0, schema v15)

## Adicionado

- The **`mapping-thread` 1** format, for a conversation anchored on a mapping item, documented in `docs/COMMENT-FORMAT.md`: one file per conversation in `comments/<id>.json` (resolved ones in `comments/resolved/<id>.json`), outside `mapping.json`, with the messages from people and agents, the nine anchors, the step-by-step for resolving a git conflict and the validity rules. It is a new format, read and written by the app and by the MCP server (items below).
- The `mapping://` **reference grammar** gains the layer (`l`) and the conversation (`c`), besides the field (`/f/`), pair (`/e/`) and row (`/r/`) segments, and the citation becomes a plain markdown link with the target's current name. Today's references (`m`, `i`, `a`, `p`) remain valid. The MCP server accepts the new forms in the tools (item below).
- **Identity** section in Settings (`Ctrl+,`): this person's name and email, stored only on this device (never in the project), which sign the conversation messages. Without them the person can only read the conversations; the email goes through a light check (`something@something`). The recommendation is to use the same ones as `git config`.
- Conversations now live in the project's storage: in the folder (`comments/` and `comments/resolved/`, checked along with `mapping.json` every few seconds), in the local project and in the **exported zip**, which carries `comments/` out and back in. Saving rereads the file and does not lose a message another program (such as the MCP server) added in the meantime. There is no screen for conversing yet: the interface arrives in the next phases of the epic.
- **Conversation in Details:** each marking, container and arrow gets the **Conversations** section, which lists the open conversations of the item and of its parts (annotations, fields, pairs and rows), shows the conversation with its messages in markdown (with line breaks), the badge of who the conversation is waiting on and **Resolve** or **Reopen**. **Ctrl+Enter** sends and Enter breaks the line; each conversation's draft survives a change of selection. Without an identity the person can only read, with the notice and the **Fill in Settings** shortcut. Layer, loose point and orphan conversation open on their own in Details. An unread conversation appears in bold.
- The **Comments** window (`Ctrl+Shift+0`, `Alt+0` where the browser allows), in the bottom window, with the columns By, Anchor, Last message, Msgs, Status and When; filters for status, user and search (ignoring case and accents); the count of open, waiting for agent and resolved; and **New conversation** anchored on the selection. The strip and the tab show how many are open.
- In the **status bar**, "N open conversations · M waiting for agent" (disappears at zero and opens the window) and **Comment** in the layer row's menu.
- **Deleting** conversations, a person's act: directly when all the messages are yours; with a dialog that says whose messages will go away when they are not; and in bulk (**Delete conversations…**, with **Only the resolved ones** and the date of the last message). In the review of a proposal, conversations are read-only.
- **Conversation pins on the canvas, hidden by default.** The **Conversations** button (canvas bar, next to View) turns the pins on and off; when off, it shows the count of conversations waiting for you and a dot if there is an unread message. The pin sits at the top right corner of the marking or container (in the middle of the arrow's route, or on the point itself), gathers the item's conversations, changes color with the state (waiting for human, waiting for agent, resolved), shows the initials and the number of messages, and opens the conversation in Details on click. The selected conversation shows its own pin even with the pins off. The pins disappear in view mode and in the review of a proposal.
- **Comment button** (main bar, next to New container): turns on the pins and arms a click on the canvas; over an item, the conversation stays on the item, and on an empty spot, on a point (which moves with the container). Esc cancels and the previous tool comes back. The canvas context menu gets **Comment** over an item and **Comment here** on an empty spot; the conversation is only saved on the first **Send**, and the draft on a point shows a dashed pin.
- **Citation in the middle of a sentence:** the conversation composer gets citation chips. Type **`@`** to search by the item's field names (the selector lists the options), or **paste** a copied reference: it becomes a chip in the text, without the raw address.
- **Cite in the conversation:** the field, pair, row, annotation, layer and conversation menus get **Cite in the conversation** (takes the reference to the composer), **Comment** (opens a conversation anchored there) and **Copy reference**. On a simple typed field, the ⋯ menu carries the same actions.
- **Ctrl+C on a property:** with a field, pair or row in focus (outside a text field), Ctrl+C copies its reference. It only works in a folder project, as with the other references.
- **Live chip:** the citation shows the target's **current name**. If the item goes away or is deleted, the chip is struck through and gray; it is still clickable.
- **Cited in:** an item's conversation lists, below the messages, the conversations that cite it, in clickable chips.
- **Citing selection:** the **Comments** window gets the **Citing selection** filter, which shows only the conversations that cite the selected item.
- **Proposal in the conversation:** the conversation shows a proposal card (title, "N change(s) to review" and the **Review** button), and the **Proposals** window marks the ones that came from a conversation, with "from the conversation" and its chip.
- **Conversations in the MCP server (0.25.0).** Five new tools: **`list_threads`** (the inbox: with no filter, the open conversations waiting for the agent; filters for state, who it is waiting on, anchor and text), **`get_thread`** (the whole conversation, with the anchor resolved, the citations resolved, `citedIn` and the linked proposals), **`open_thread`** (opens a conversation anchored on any reference or on a loose canvas point, to ask when the specialization does not decide), **`post_message`** (replies in an open conversation; `after` is required and a message that came in the meantime gives `stale-thread`, saving nothing) and **`wait_for_comment`** (waits up to 50 s for a conversation that starts waiting for the agent; the deadline comes from the spike on Claude Code's 60 s limit, in `docs/MCP.md`). The server only writes `comments/<id>.json`, always as an agent and without an email, and **never resolves, reopens or deletes** a conversation. New errors: `thread-not-found`, `thread-resolved`, `stale-thread`, `invalid-anchor`, `anchor-not-found`, `empty-message`, `message-too-long` and `invalid-thread`; new warnings: `broken-cite` and `thread-not-linked`.
- **`propose_changes` with `thread`:** the proposal is linked to a conversation. The conversation is checked before saving (nonexistent or resolved: refused, leaving nothing on disk); after the proposal is saved, the conversation receives an agent message with the proposal card (**Review** in the app).
- **References with the new grammar in the tools:** `resolve` understands layer (`l`), conversation (`c`), field (`/f/`), pair (`/e/`) and row (`/r/`) and returns the `kind` `layer`, `thread`, `field`, `entry` or `row`; `get_annotation` and the other item tools accept field, pair and row like the annotation that contains them. In a marking or container tool, the part is `wrong-kind`.
- **Resource `mapping-docs://COMMENT-FORMAT.md`** (the conversation format) and the **Conversations** section of the agent guide: when to open a conversation, the `after`, citing instead of repeating, linking the proposal with `thread`, answering only what is asked of it, the `wait_for_comment` loop.
- **`watch-comments.mjs`** (`examples/workspace/`): wakes the agent when a conversation starts waiting for it. It watches `projetos/*/comments/`, waits 5 s without change, runs the command you pass after `--` (one at a time; each conversation and last message is handled once) and delivers the conversations in `MAPPING_AWAITING`. The MCP server never triggers an agent; `setup.sh` does not update the file.
- **Example and guide with conversations.** `examples/exemplo-completo/` (and the zip) gets `comments/` with three example conversations (one waiting for you, on the **estilo** field of **Cadastrar**; one waiting for the agent, on **Salvar**; and a resolved one), with made-up names and emails; the interactive guide gets a text step about the conversations and the **Comments** window (`Ctrl+Shift+0`), and now has 13 steps.
- **Script R22 — Comments** in `docs/TESTING.md`, for the cloud tester to run on its own: identity, window and filters, hidden pins and the **Conversations** and **Comment** buttons, conversation on the item, on the field and on the loose point, the conversation tools through the stdio client, the proposal card, resolving and reopening, citation, orphan, deleting and the zip; it joins block 3 of the full round and the **Comments** window joins the visual pass.
