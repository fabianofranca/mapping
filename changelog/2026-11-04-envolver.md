# Wrap — 2026-11-04 (epic `epic/envolver`, PR #461, MCP 0.14.0, schema v13)

## Adicionado

- **Ctrl+G (Cmd+G on macOS) wraps the selection** in a new marking, with one or more items, in a single undo entry; the new marking stays selected, as with the **Wrap** button of the summary. The shortcut does not act (and the browser goes on with "find next") with focus in a text field, in a read-only project, in proposal review, with no selection or when there are only notes and arrows. It enters the Help shortcuts list and the tooltip of the **Wrap** button.
- **Wrap in the canvas context menu and in the Tree's ⋯ menu**, with the shortcut beside it: on the canvas, it wraps the current selection; in the row's ⋯, the selection if the item is in it, otherwise only the row's item. Disabled, with the tooltip, when nothing fits in (a note); without the entry on the arrow, in read-only and in review.
- **Wrap with a single item**: a lone marking or container can be wrapped by the shortcut and the menus, with the same result as today (8 px margin, limited to the container and the current parent).
- **MCP 0.14.0: `wrap_items` operation** in `plan_changes` and `propose_changes`: creates a marking around markings and containers (`items`), the same result as the app's **Wrap**, with `as` (an alias for the batch's following operations), `name` and `source` optional. Notes and arrows in the list are ignored with an `ignored-argument` notice; with nothing that fits in, it fails with `invalid-parent` and a `detail`. The review shows the creation of a marking, like a `create_marking`.

## Corrigido

- **Holding Ctrl+G (Cmd+G) does not stack parents:** the repeated key does not wrap again (before, each repetition wrapped the new marking that had just become selected) and the shortcut does not act with a drag or resize in progress (before, the notice "Couldn’t create the parent marking" appeared in the middle of the gesture). The browser's "find next" stays out of the way.
- **Wrap on the image outside a folder:** the image row's ⋯ in the Tree and the right-click on the image show **Wrap** (the image is a container) also in a project opened outside a folder, without bringing the copy entries, which depend on a folder.
- **MCP: `wrap_items` on a rectangle tie.** When the wrapped item fills the container, the new marking gets the same rectangle as it and, when applying the proposal, the hierarchy may come out inverted from the plan's (the new one a child of the item). The behavior did not change; the documentation (`docs/MCP.md`, `docs/AGENT-GUIDE.md`, `examples/workspace/AGENTS.md`) now says so and a test pins it.
- **Scripts R12.13 and R12.20a** (`docs/TESTING.md`): R12.13 says to clear the note's fields (which is born with "Title" and "Write the comment here.") before typing and cites the right text of the **Components** tab; R12.20a describes what `wrap_items` does with markings of different containers (it wraps the containers).
