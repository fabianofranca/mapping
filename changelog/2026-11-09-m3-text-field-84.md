# Material 3: text-field with height 84 — 2026-11-09 (epic `epic/m3-text-field-84`, PR #1235, MCP 0.28.11 (no new version), schema v18)

## Corrigido

- Material 3 (Compose) library, `version` 3: the `text-field` and the `secure-text-field` now have a height of 84 in `size` and `sizing` (it was 76). The `outlined` field with supporting text, like the "Em erro" example, takes 84 in Compose (8 for the label + 56 for the outline + 4 + 16 for the support) and was 8 px past the box. The width and the minimum size do not change.
- Effect: the drawing always has the height of `size`, so the `filled` with support is left with 8 empty px at the bottom and the `filled` without support with 28. Markings already created do not migrate: they keep their own rectangle and now draw 84 inside it.
- Test guide (`docs/TESTING.md`), script R17, findings of the tester round: R17.2 passes `root` `design/mapeamentos` in `create_project` (section 6 has two roots); R17.13 checks the ink of the supporting text by counting the `#B3261E` pixels with `grep -c` (the previous check printed `1` even on a strip with no ink); R17.10 describes the empty-drawing mode for `dark` and the Scaffold background; R17.9 tells you to pick the library in the Catalog's **Library** selector; R17.13 warns that the **Erro** marking overlaps the **Cancelar** one.
