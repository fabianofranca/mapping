# Supervision: resume without arguments and handoff in a database — 2026-11-11 (épico `epic/supervisao-retomada`, PR #1672, MCP 0.35.1 unchanged, schema v18)

## Adicionado

- `painel.py roles --sessao <id> <pasta>` (skill `implementar-fila`, `painel.py`): besides the usual two lines, it prints `esta sessão: …`, checked in this order (the first that matches wins): `registrada` (the `roles.supervisor.id`), `coordenador (não supervisiona)` (the `roles.coordenador.id` or `.previous`, or `kind: coordenador` in `meta.sessions`), `antiga (atual: <id>)` (the `roles.supervisor.previous`, or `kind: supervisor` in `meta.sessions`), `sucessora (antecessor: <id>)` (another id, with a registered supervisor) or `nova (nenhum supervisor registrado)`. An empty id exits with code 2. A session knows its role from the script's output, without reading the JSON, and the old one in which the owner writes `/supervisionar-fila` by mistake does not find itself the successor.
- `painel.py requests --desde <ISO> <pasta>`: prints, in the usual format, only the requests with `at` from the requested time on and all the open ones (`enviado`, `perguntado ao dono`, `aprovado`); with requests left out, the last line says `<k> pedidos fechados antes de <ISO> fora da lista`, and with nothing to show, `nenhum desde <ISO>`. Supervision stops rereading the whole table every turn. Without `--desde`, the output is the same as before.
- Page `.claude/skills/supervisionar-fila/passagem/index.html`: the "Supervisão da fila" artifact becomes this page, published with the `db` capability, which only reads the `passagem` collection and shows the rules grouped under "Decisões do dono", "Issues abertas", "Agendamentos" and "Lições" (with `texto`, `origem` and `desde`, and `trigger` and `parada` in the schedules). If the database does not respond in 10 s, it says that the handoff did not load.

## Alterado

- Skill `supervisionar-fila`: `/supervisionar-fila` without arguments is the normal form. The session finds the panel (`Artifact` `list` with the titles "Estado da execução", "Fila de desenvolvimento" and "Mapping Fila", the `pinned` first) and decides its role by the `esta sessão:` line of `painel.py roles --sessao`: `registrada`, `sucessora`, `nova`, `antiga` or `coordenador` (the last two do not act and answer only with the link of the current supervisor). `retomar`, `anterior=<id>`, `painel=<url>`, `custo` and `varredura desde=<ISO>` remain valid.
- Supervision session swap: the ~150k criterion and the ~25% limit go away. The normal swap is the owner's when coming back after more than 1 h (a new session, without spending a lineage slot). The base is the `used_tokens` at the end of the first turn (120 thousand without it); from 2× the base the answer ends with the reminder to the owner; the automatic swap is only above ~50% (~500k), without a handoff turn, and the successor recreates the predecessor's schedules and archives it.
- Supervision handoff in the database of the same artifact, one rule per document of the `passagem` collection (`decisao`, `issue`, `agendamento` or `licao`), read with `ArtifactData` `list` and written with `if_version` at each owner decision, without republishing the page or loading `artifact-design`. The first supervisor registered after the merge migrates the Markdown handoff to the database at the same link.
- Reading of each supervision turn: the panel's `meta` goes to a file (`out_dir`) and never enters the context, the requests come from `painel.py requests --desde`, the predecessor's `get_session` is read once and the coordinator's only when the owner's question asks for it. Every subagent briefing asks for the return in up to 10 lines.
- `docs/FLOW.md`, "Supervisão da fila": opens and resumes with `/supervisionar-fila` without arguments, a new session when coming back after more than 1 h and an automatic swap only above ~50%.
