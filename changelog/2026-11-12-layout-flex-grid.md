# Layout aligned with Flexbox and CSS Grid — 2026-11-12 (épico `epic/layout-flex-grid`, PR #1676, MCP 0.36.0, schema v18)

## Adicionado

- **Aligned overlay.** A frame with `layout: { "type": "none" }` accepts `align` (vertical axis) and `justify` (horizontal axis) with `start`, `center`, `end` and `stretch`, written directly or by expression. It is Compose's `Box(contentAlignment)`, CSS Grid's `place-items` and SwiftUI's `ZStack(alignment:)`: a badge centered on an icon no longer needs `anchors` arithmetic. The child's `anchors` still apply and win over the alignment on the axis where they exist. Only in the component's `formatVersion` 4; before it, the error is `só é permitido com formatVersion 4`.
- **`clip` on the slot and by expression.** The slot gains `clip` (default `false`): with it, the child markings drawn inside the slot are clipped by the slot's box, in the app, in the viewer and in the MCP PNG (`render_container`, `render_marking`). The frame's `clip` also accepts an expression. There is no scrolling. Only in the component's `formatVersion` 4.
- **Grid.** The frame, the slot and the `repeat` gain `layout: { "type": "grid" }`, like Compose's `LazyVerticalGrid` and CSS's `grid-template-columns`: `columns` (equal columns), `minColumnWidth` (as many columns as fit, the `GridCells.Adaptive`) and, in the horizontal grid, `rows` and `minRowHeight`, with `gap` between the columns and `lineGap` between the rows. The child accepts `span` to occupy neighboring columns (what does not fit at the end of the row goes to the next one, leaving the hole), `justify` and `align` apply inside the cell (`stretch` included) and a `hug` grid measures equal columns by the widest cell. Only in the component's `formatVersion` 4; before it, the error is `só é permitido com formatVersion 4`.
- **`repeat.layout`.** The `repeat` accepts a full `layout` (`row`, `column` or `grid`) in place of `direction` + `gap`: the segments of a segmented button, for example, become a `hug` grid of equal columns (`columns` equal to the number of rows of the table).
- **Weights.** A `fill` child of `row` or `column` accepts `weight` (Compose's `Modifier.weight`, CSS's `flex-grow`): the slack is split in proportion to the weights, and a child that hits `maxWidth` or `minWidth` freezes and the difference goes to the others.
- **The grid also arranges markings.** A grid slot is arranged by Arrange and, on a screen without geometry, by `layoutScreen`: each child marking occupies a cell, and the new child enters the next free cell. The `weight` of the child markings of a slot does not exist.
- **`min-content` and `max-content`.** The width (and height) of a component node and the instance's `sizing` accept `"min-content"` (the widest word of the text, the largest unbreakable piece of the row, the column or the grid) and `"max-content"` (the content without breaking: the `row` with `wrap` stays on a single line), as in CSS and in Compose's `IntrinsicSize`. Unlike `hug`, they are not limited to the parent's space (if it overflows, the `conteúdo maior que a caixa` notice comes out as usual); in height, both behave as `hug`. The screen without geometry, Arrange and the MCP's `fit_marking` follow the `sizing` with these values. Only in the component's `formatVersion` 4; before it, the error is `só é permitido com formatVersion 4`, and the `image` node does not accept them.
- **Arrange aligns the children of a `none` slot.** The slot `layout: { "type": "none" }` with `align` and `justify` puts each child alone in the slot's box (`start`, `center`, `end` and `stretch`) when the person asks for **Arrange**, and the screen without geometry does the same on the axes of known size, like Compose's `Box(contentAlignment)`; without them, nothing changes.
- **Fixed or adaptive columns by a field.** In a grid with `type` by expression or in `repeat`/slot, `columns` and `minColumnWidth` (or `rows` and `minRowHeight`) can appear together when all of them are expressions, and the first one that has a value applies: this is how a type chooses between `GridCells.Fixed` and `GridCells.Adaptive` by a field.
- `docs/COMPONENT-FORMAT.md` gains the concept map (overlay, row and column, reversed order, wrapping, clipping) with the equivalent keys of CSS, Compose, SwiftUI and Flutter.

## Alterado

- `dist/index.html` ceiling raised by 16 KiB, from 2,080,768 to 2,097,152, by the budget rule (the grid, the weights and the new L3 messages).
- Component drawing got faster when measuring repeated text: the same text, with the same font and the same width limit, is measured only once, in the app and in the MCP server (in the 15 components of the Material 3 example, the measures per instance dropped from 69 to 26). No drawing changes.
- The new layout costs no performance: a whole example screen (21 Material 3 components, with aligned overlay, grid, weights and `max-content`) still fits in the same budgets as before (16 ms to instantiate and draw, 24 ms to redo the whole frame), and moving or zooming the canvas does not re-instantiate any component.
- **Material 3 (Compose) v9 uses the new layout.** The library update is additive (no migration; no field changed):
  - `box` aligns the children by `contentAlignment` in Arrange (`Center`, `BottomEnd`…); with `height` `fill` or `fixed` the marking's height stays (this is where the vertical alignment shows);
  - `lazy-vertical-grid` draws the children in a real grid: `columns` (`GridCells.Fixed`) or `minColumnWidth` (`GridCells.Adaptive`), `horizontalSpacing` between the columns and `verticalSpacing` between the rows; `contentPadding` moved from the frame to the slot, inside the clipped box;
  - `segmented-button` has segments of equal width by the widest one (Compose's `IntrinsicSize.Min`): at the fitted size the width is `n` times the widest segment (before, the `size` of 328 applied);
  - `lazy-column`, `lazy-row`, `lazy-vertical-grid`, `row` and `column` clip the child that goes past the component's box (`clipToBounds`), in the app and in the MCP PNG. The reference images of these five and of `box` and `segmented-button` changed on purpose.

## Corrigido

- The drawing proof and the library validation (#1093) now warn `conteúdo maior que a caixa: <quadro>` also when the parent of the child that overflows is `layout: none`, as they already did in `row` and `column`. The notice appears in the app's "the component does not fit" pending item, in the `component-overflow` of `plan_changes` and `propose_changes` and in the `validate_library` notices.
- In the app, a word wider than the text box stays whole on the line (as in the model and in the MCP PNG) instead of breaking letter by letter; the false "component does not fit" notice on narrow one-word buttons goes away.
- The drawing proof sees the components applied in the same `plan_changes` batch.
- The `segmented-button` fitted with a width limit (`fit_marking`) no longer shrinks below the widest label.
- The `card` clips the media with the card's own rounded corners, and the stuck-together strips of a `row` no longer show a light thread between them in the app.
- `hug` grid with a wrong track count in `fill` frames and an empty `hug` grid measured differently by the slot arrangement.
- The `segmented-button` near the container's edge (`fit_marking` and Arrange only receive the width that is left) no longer has a label overlapping its neighbor: the label shrinks inside the track with `…` and the row does not report `conteúdo maior que a caixa`. In general, text with `ellipsis` in a `row` yields the excess instead of going past the box.
- A frame with a shadow or stroke outside the box and a rounded corner (`box`, `surface`, `card` with elevation) clips the content by the shape, in the app and in the MCP PNG: the corner of a child that touches it no longer comes out square.
