# Content-based measurement in format 4 — 2026-11-11 (épico `epic/medida-conteudo`, PR #1580, MCP 0.35.0, schema v18)

## Adicionado

- Components (format 4): `baselinePadding` (`top` and `bottom`) on the `text` node positions the text by the baseline, like Compose's `paddingFromBaseline`, and `layout.align: "baseline"` aligns the children of a `row` (also in the `repeat` and in the slot, and in Arrange) by the first baseline. The app and the MCP PNG draw the baseline in the same place (≤ 0.5 px, in any font and zoom). The text meters now report `firstBaseline`, `lastBaseline` and `lineCount`.
- Components (format 4): `{ "$lines": "<text node>" }` is the number of measured lines of a text, so the height depends on the content (the snackbar of 48 or 68, for example). The engine lays out in two passes and warns `$lines instável: <nó>` when the number changes from one to the other. The `sizing` accepts an expression (`$switch` by the style, `$if` by a field) that gives px, `fill` or `hug`, on the screen without geometry, in the catalog playground and in the Arrange of a component with a slot; an invalid value falls back to the `size` with a warning. The library validator's drawing proof also draws each case at the size fitted to the content (`tamanho ajustado W×H` in the warning).
- Components: **Arrange** (context menu, Details button and `Alt+Shift+A`) also applies to a component marking without a slot whose `sizing` depends on the content (`hug` or an expression): a button with a long label grows in height, with the top-left corner in place, between the `minSize` and the root's limits, without going past the image; `Ctrl+Z` undoes it in a single entry. Editing a value still does not change the rectangle. The new instance (without `rect`, in the app and in the MCP `create_marking`) is already born at the size measured with the `preview` and the given values.
- MCP: the `fit_marking` (`marking`) operation of `plan_changes` and `propose_changes` makes the same adjustment in the batch and in the proposal; the summary gives the rectangle before and after (or "nothing changes"), and the proposal review shows the adjustment as the **Position** change, which can be accepted or rejected separately from the value. Errors `not-fittable` and `locked`. The agents' guide says to call it after changing a value that alters the content of a fittable component, also when reviewing a proposal and when answering a conversation.
- Components (format 4): `layout.wrap` (`"wrap"` or `"wrap-reverse"`) breaks a row into lines, like CSS's `flex-wrap` and Compose's `FlowRow`, with `lineGap` (space between the lines), `maxPerLine` and `maxLines` (the children beyond the last line are not drawn, with the warning `<n> filhos fora de maxLines: <nó>`). It applies in a `row`/`column` frame, in the slot and in the `repeat` (the four keys on the node itself), and `justify`, `reverse` and `align` (including `baseline`) apply on each line. In the slot, **Arrange** puts the child markings in lines and the new child enters the next line when it does not fit. The app, the viewer and the MCP PNG draw the lines in the same place (≤ 0.5 px).

The snackbar measures 48, 68 and 81 with one, two and three lines; the list-item with two-line support measures 88 with the content pinned to the top; the top-app-bar measures 64, 112 or 152 by the `style` on fit; the button, the chip, the extended-fab and the buttons of the alert-dialog with a long label break the label and grow in height by the lines; the text-field and the secure-text-field measure the height by the support text (the filled one without support no longer has 28 empty below) and the search-bar, the text-field and the secure-text-field gain `minWidth`/`maxWidth` on the root, which apply to the measured width.

The alert-dialog buttons go to the line below when they do not fit, with the confirm one above (the `AlertDialogFlowRow`), and each one sits in a frame with a 48 touch area. The dropdown-menu's menu measures the width by the widest item, between 112 and 280, when fitted to the content (the exposed field keeps following the marking's width), and the item's text sits between the icons. New examples: "Três por linha", "Botões em duas linhas" and "Itens de larguras diferentes".
