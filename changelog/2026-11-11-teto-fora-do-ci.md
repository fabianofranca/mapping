# dist/index.html ceiling outside ci.yml — 2026-11-11 (epic `epic/teto-fora-do-ci`, PR #1648, MCP unchanged, schema v18)

## Alterado

- The `dist/index.html` ceiling and the history of the raises leave the `env:` of the `build` job in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` and move to `scripts/ci/dist-budget.json` (`{ "indexHtmlMaxBytes": <bytes>, "history": [<sentences>] }`, checked by Prettier). Raising the ceiling no longer edits `.github/` (Auto mode treated editing the workflow as sensitive and denied it).
- `node scripts/ci/distBudget.mjs raise` loses `--ci <ci.yml>` (refused with the message "--ci saiu na etapa #1614…", to catch the session that still follows the old text) and gains `--budget <file>`, defaulting to `scripts/ci/dist-budget.json`. With `--write` and "sobe: …", it swaps the `indexHtmlMaxBytes` and appends the raise's sentence to the end of `history`. New command: `ceiling [--budget <file>]` prints the ceiling. The limits (14 KiB of excess, 32 KiB of growth, 64 KiB since the revision), the 16 KiB raise and the script's sentences do not change.
- The "Tamanho do dist/index.html" step of `ci.yml` reads the ceiling through `node scripts/ci/distBudget.mjs ceiling`; on a ref earlier than this stage (`deploy.yml` runs the `main` `ci.yml` against the preview commit), it reads the `INDEX_HTML_MAX_BYTES: <n>` line from the checkout's `ci.yml`; without both, it fails with the `::error` for ceiling not found. The summary, the `::error` and the `::warning` stay the same.
- `docs/FLOW.md` ("For the phase session", item 6, "`dist/index.html` ceiling"), the `implementar-fila` skill ("Red CI on the epic PR") and `README.md` cite `scripts/ci/dist-budget.json`, without the `--ci`. New transition rule: a branch earlier than this stage that raised the ceiling in `ci.yml` resolves the conflict by keeping the `main` `ci.yml` and copies the raise (the number and the sentence) to `scripts/ci/dist-budget.json`.
- `scripts/ci/dist-budget.json` does not enter `riskTriggers.mjs`: an epic whose only CI change is the ceiling raise no longer gets the closing `/code-review high` just for that.
