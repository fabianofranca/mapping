# Usage and quality per role in the queue — [Não lançado] (épico `epic/qualidade-por-papel`, PR #0, MCP 0.41.0 unchanged, schema v19)

## Adicionado

- **The queue panel computes the usage per role × model × effort on every write (for whoever drives the flow, #1889, QM1):** `meta.uso` has one entry per key of the sessions with a cost: sessions, US$, share of the total, median US$ per session and per merged stage, median context, sessions above the 300 thousand ceiling, interventions (the owner's questions and the context switches), the rework count and the clean streak. `painel.py uso [--desde <ISO>] <folder>` prints one line per key and the signals added up.
- **Quality signals of each session (for whoever drives the flow, #1889, QM1):** `painel.py sinal <folder> <id> field=value…` (also inside `apply`) writes `meta.sessions[i].sinais`: `ci`, `cr`, `crFix`, `ciclo`, `dono` and `sub` for phase and fix sessions; `ok`, `falha`, `alta`, `media`, `baixa`, `falso` and `sub` for tester and retest sessions. A plain number replaces, `+k` adds, and a field of another role, an unknown field, a negative number or text where a number is expected exits with code 2 and writes nothing.
- **Escaped defects and the history marker (for whoever drives the flow, #1889, QM1):** `painel.py escapado <folder> '#<bug>' etapa='#<n>' sev=<high|medium|low>` writes `meta.escapados` (the same bug replaces, no duplicate) and `escapado <folder> --conferido` writes `meta.escapadosAt`; `painel.py historico <folder> --feito desde=<ISO> ate=<ISO> [linhas=<k>]` writes `meta.historico`.
- **Three new lines in `painel.py show` (for whoever drives the flow, #1889, QM1):** `escapados:` (the defects and when they were last checked, with `— conferir` after 24 h or without a check), `histórico:` (`a recuperar` or `recuperado (…)`) and `candidatos a teste:` (the keys with 10 decided sessions in a row without rework, each with the configuration one step below: an effort above `medium` goes down one step; with `medium` or `low` the model goes down one).
- **The rework ruler (for whoever drives the flow, #1889, QM1):** only sessions of a decided stage count (`concluída` or in `meta.merged`); the rule of each role (phase, fix, tester, retest) is in the module docstring of `painel.py`, and a missing signal is "no data", not rework.
- **The history is recovered once, in the new session (for whoever drives the flow, #1889, QM2):** `implementar-fila` ("Session switch" › "In the new session", step 5) sends, when the `show` says `histórico: a recuperar`, a Sonnet `high` subagent that reads the `log` from its first record, the merged PRs and the `bug` issues, writes the batch of `sinal`, `escapado` and `log` lines to a file and returns one line; the coordinator applies it with `painel.py apply` and closes it with `historico --feito`. The text was rehearsed on a read-only copy of the live panel before this phase merged.

## Alterado

- **The final notices carry the signals of quality (for whoever drives the flow, #1889, QM2):** the phase and fix notice (item 7 of "For the phase session" in `docs/FLOW.md`) gains, before `Contexto:`, the lines `CI vermelho depois do PR: <k>`, `Revisão: <N> achados, <X> corrigidos` (or `Revisão: não se aplica`) and `Subagentes: <n> <modelo> <esforço>, …` (or `Subagentes: nenhum`); the tester and retest notice (`docs/TESTING-NUVEM.md`, "Report", item 5) gains `; subagentes <n> <modelo> <esforço>, …` (or `; subagentes: nenhum`), before the duration and the final `; contexto <n> mil`, in the three formats.
- **The coordinator records the signals (for whoever drives the flow, #1889, QM2):** `implementar-fila` writes `painel.py sinal` in the same `apply` as each notice (`ci`, `cr`, `crFix`, `sub` for phase and fix; `ok`, `falha`, `alta`, `media`, `baixa`, `sub` for tester and retest), `dono=+1` when a phase waits for the owner, `ciclo=+1` when the CI-cycle diagnosis says it is a cycle, and `falso=<sev>` when the fix briefing returns a finding on a step a retest had passed. The notice of an epic older than this stage records only what came.
- **The escaped defects are checked once a day (for whoever drives the flow, #1889, QM2):** when `painel.py show` says `escapados: … — conferir`, the hourly check sends a subagent that lists the `bug` issues created after the `ended` of the stage that cites them and records them with `escapado` and `escapado --conferido`.
- **`supervisionar-fila` reads the usage (for whoever drives the flow, #1889, QM2):** the `custo` request also answers with `painel.py uso <folder>`, per role, model and effort, with the rework and the clean streak.
