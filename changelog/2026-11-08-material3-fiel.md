# Faithful Material 3 (Compose) — 2026-11-08 (epic `epic/material3-fiel`, PR #860, MCP 0.28.0 (only the arc raster fix, no new version), schema v18)

## Adicionado

- The Material 3 (Compose) library has a **dark theme**: the specialization moves to `formatVersion` 15 (library version 2) and declares the `tokens` in the `light` and `dark` modes, with all the color roles of the Compose baseline (`primary`, `on-primary`, `surface-container-high`…), the 15 typography scales, the shape, the elevation and the state, taken from the androidx token files at the pinned commit. With the project mode set to `dark`, the migrated components draw the dark colors.
- The library brings an **icon set** (`icons/material-icons.json`): 164 Material Icons Filled icons (Compose's `Icons.Default`), from the `@material-design-icons/svg` 0.14.15 package, with the Apache 2.0 license and the source alongside. The suggestions for icon fields now list the whole set, with the Compose name (`Home`, `KeyboardArrowRight`).

## Alterado

- `text`, `icon`, `divider` and `surface` move to component format 4 and become faithful to Compose: `text` reads the typography tokens and inherits Roboto (no `family` in the component) and cuts at `maxLines` like `Text`; `icon` draws the set's icon in place of a `$switch` of SVG paths, and a name outside the set draws the placeholder and warns; `divider` accepts any thickness; `surface` applies tonal elevation only over the `surface` color, as Compose does. No literal color remains in these four.
- The documents of these four types gain the **Fidelity** section (Compose token files used and what still differs).
- Updating the library from version 1 to 2 is additive: no field changes key, type or options, and the applied projects update without migration.
- The 19 **layout and actions** components (`scaffold`, `box`, `row`, `column`, `lazy-column`, `lazy-row`, `lazy-vertical-grid`, `flow-row`, `spacer`, `horizontal-pager`, `button`, `fab`, `extended-fab`, `icon-button`, `icon-toggle-button`, `chip`, `badge`, `card` and `image`) move to format 4 and become faithful to Compose: color tokens in both modes (no literal color), `corner-*` radii, typography and Roboto through the tokens, icon through the `icon` node in place of SVG paths and the **Fidelity** section in each document. `row`, `column`, `lazy-column`, `lazy-row`, `flow-row` and `horizontal-pager` now apply the chosen distribution and alignment; `button` is 40 high and fully rounded; `chip`, `fab` and `extended-fab` follow the colors of each Compose style and state.
- The `button` label no longer has `family` and inherits the font of the `font-sans` token.
- Limits left for later (they become `roadmap` at closing): `shadow.y` and `shadow.blur` do not accept `$token` (elevation levels enter as numbers equal to the tokens); `SpaceAround` and `SpaceEvenly` draw as `space-between`; `reverseLayout` and the `flow-row` line wrapping do not exist; the library validator does not pass the tokens to the proof drawing.
- The 9 **selection and input** components (`checkbox`, `radio-button`, `switch`, `slider`, `text-field`, `secure-text-field`, `search-bar`, `segmented-button` and `progress-indicator`) move to format 4 and become faithful to Compose: color tokens in both modes (no literal color), radii, typography and Roboto through the tokens, icons through the `icon` node and the **Fidelity** section in each document. The filled `text-field` draws the indicator only on the bottom border; `segmented-button` divides the width into equal segments; the disabled `switch` composes the colors over `surface`; the circular `progress-indicator` is 40 and has Compose's round ends, and `slider` draws track, thumb, ticks and stop as Compose does. The `checkbox` box is 20 (Compose's `CheckboxSize`) and the `radio-button` dot is 10.
- Limits left for later (they become `roadmap` at closing): `cornerRadius` in a four-value list does not accept `$token`; numeric arithmetic and comparison are missing in expressions (`progress-indicator` and `slider` use case tables); `anchors` do not accept an expression; `minWidth`/`maxWidth` at the root have no effect; there is no outline with a rounded gap (the outlined `text-field` with a label) or color composition over another; no focus and pressed states.
- The 9 **navigation and lists** components (`top-app-bar`, `bottom-app-bar`, `navigation-bar`, `short-navigation-bar`, `navigation-rail`, `navigation-drawer`, `tabs`, `list-item` and `dropdown-menu`) move to format 4 and become faithful to Compose: color tokens in both modes (no literal color), typography and Roboto through the tokens, icons through the `icon` node, destinations and tabs of equal width by `repeat.distribute: "fill"`, one-side border by `stroke.sides`, top bar title and `list-item` texts cut with `…` like the composable, minimum heights by expression and the **Fidelity** section in each document. The active destination indicator of `navigation-bar` and of the rail becomes 56 × 32 (Compose 1.4.0's `ActiveIndicatorWidth`; before it was 64), and the secondary tab indicator is 3 high.
- Limits left for later (they become `roadmap` at closing): `shadow.y` and `shadow.blur` do not accept `$token`, and there is one shadow per node; `cornerRadius` in a list does not accept `$token`; text baseline alignment is missing (medium/large bar title and tab with the icon above); `sizing` by expression or measured by content and a layout that reacts to the measured number of lines are missing (the supporting `list-item` with 2 lines without an overline); counting the rows of a table in an expression is missing (centered margin of `short-navigation-bar`); intrinsic width for the menu and tab label wrapping are missing.
- The 9 **surfaces and pickers** components (`alert-dialog`, `bottom-sheet`, `snackbar`, `tooltip`, `date-picker`, `time-picker`, `carousel`, `pull-to-refresh` and `swipe-to-dismiss`) move to format 4 and become faithful to Compose: color tokens in both modes (no literal color), radii, typography and Roboto through the tokens, icons through the `icon` node and the **Fidelity** section in each document. With them, **all 50 components of the library are in format 4**, with no literal color and no `family`, and draw without warnings in the `light` and `dark` modes. `date-picker` and `time-picker` drop from 120 kB and 98 kB to 78 kB and 53 kB. Visible differences: `alert-dialog` buttons 40 high; `bottom-sheet` handle without the 0.4 opacity; `snackbar` in `inverse-surface` with a level 3 shadow; the `swipe-to-dismiss` label comes out 56 (the `positionalThreshold`); the empty icon of `swipe-to-dismiss` becomes `delete` and a name outside the set warns `unknown icon`; `carousel` reads `itemWidth` and `preferredItemWidth`; the `time-picker` clock numbers have no leading zero.
- Limits left for later (they become `roadmap` at closing): `repeat` only walks a `table` field (no count or row index); `anchors` does not accept an expression; numeric arithmetic and comparison are missing in expressions (31-case tables in `date-picker`, 60-case in `time-picker`); no trigonometry or rotation by value; `$token` without an alpha channel; `shadow.y`/`blur` and `cornerRadius` in a list without `$token` and one shadow per node; no baseline alignment; no layout line wrapping (`AlertDialogFlowRow`) or height by the measured number of lines; the root's `min`/`max` only apply in measurement without a rectangle; `sizing` without expression; a field for "today" in `date-picker`.
- **New R17 steps** (R17.10 to R17.12): the project's dark mode, the same screen in both modes and the set's icon drawn instead of the placeholder.

## Corrigido

- **Catalog:** the **Gallery** and the **Playground** of a component's page now draw with the specialization's tokens and icon set (the filled **Button** comes out purple with white text, the outlined one with only an outline, and "With icon" shows the `Add`, instead of gray with "unknown icon: add").
- **MCP:** `render_container` and `render_marking` draw the icon arcs (SVG `A`) instead of a straight line to the end point; the `Search` magnifier comes out with the lens hollow, as on the canvas.
- **Scripts (`docs/TESTING.md`):** the `tokens.mjs` of R25.22 merges the four tokens into the package's (`m3-tokens` validates without error); the counts of R25.23 (`m3-tokens-v14`: 2 errors), R25.43 (`m3-tela-v3`: 6 errors) and R25.44 (`m3-tela-st9`: 3 errors, with `formatVersion` 14), and the comparison of R25.26 (the package's `font-sans`, Roboto) now check out; R9.11 says that the **Components** section of Diagnostics only appears when there is something to show. A test (`tests/tooling/testingGuideR25.test.ts`) runs these steps against the package.
