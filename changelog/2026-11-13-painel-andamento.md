# Queue progress on the panel — 2026-11-13 (epic `epic/painel-andamento`, PR #1863, MCP 0.39.1 unchanged, schema v19)

## Alterado

- **The queue panel shows progress on a per-wave track, with the phases in each card (for whoever drives the flow, #1840, PA1):** the "Passo a passo" table and the "Dependências e ordem de entrada" graph become the "Andamento" card, a single list from top to bottom: "Agora" and then "Onda 1", "Onda 2"… Each card carries the issue number, the order badge, the state as text, the title, the type, the flow, the PR link, the `CI:` that the coordinator is waiting on, a chip per phase (merged, open or not yet opened), the progress bar, the current step or the "espera #n" (a solid label for a dependency, a dashed one for a shared file) and, on those already started, the live duration and the cost. The SVG arrows go away: a tap on the card highlights what it waits for and what waits for it, and the help line says which ones; another tap undoes it. The completed ones move down to the "Concluídas" card, a list per day (Brasília time), the most recent day open, with number, title, type, PR, merge time, duration and cost. The page still only reads what `painel.py` already writes, and the top, the "Custo da fila", the requests, the rules and the log stay as they were.
- **The "Andamento" card highlights the "agora" phase and the reason for the wait again, and the cost gets 7 neutral tones (for whoever drives the flow, #1840):** the first open phase of a stage in progress gets the "agora" highlight (also in the text and in the `title`), the card of wave 1 or later with incoming arrows shows the "espera #n" and the reason when it is not just `dep …`, the 7 session types of the "Custo da fila" get distinct neutral tones without reusing the state colors, and the `implementar-fila` skill describes the `CI:` on the card, not in a column that no longer exists.
