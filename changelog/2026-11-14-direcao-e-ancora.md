# Reading direction (RTL) and anchor between markings — 2026-11-14 (epic `epic/direcao-e-ancora`, PR #1859, MCP 0.40.0, schema v20)

## Adicionado

- **Reading direction in component drawing.** The engine now draws right to left (`rtl`) when its caller asks for it: the row (`row`) and each line of a wrap (`wrap`) run from right to left, the column aligns `start` to the right, the grid fills the tracks from right to left and the aligned `none` frame mirrors. `reverse` composes with the direction, as in CSS. The vertical axis and `padding` do not change, and in `ltr` no drawing today changes.
- **Logical keys in the component format** (`formatVersion` 4): `anchors.start` and `anchors.end` (the edge where reading starts and the opposite one; `left` and `right` remain physical), `font.align` with `start` and `end` (and the default going from `left` to `start`, the same drawing in `ltr`), and `layout.align`/`layout.justify` `start`/`end` now following the direction on the horizontal axis.
- **Mirrored icons:** the icon file (`mapping-icons`) goes to `formatVersion` 2, with `mirror` per icon (1 is still accepted); the `icon` and `path` nodes gain `mirror`, which in `rtl` flips the drawing horizontally. Only what the mold marks mirrors.
- **Direction per node and condition:** any node can fix `direction` (`ltr` or `rtl`) for its own subtree (a phone number, a snippet of code), and the condition `{ "$direction": "rtl" }` holds wherever a condition holds (`$if`, `visible`, `$and`, `$or`, `$not`).
- **Screens without geometry and viewer:** the screen (and the `screen` of a source) gains `direction`; the anchors `top-start`, `start` and `bottom-start` become logical (in `rtl`, on the right), and the embeddable viewer accepts `direction`, with a selector in the demo.
- **Reading direction in the project and in the container (schema v20).** `direction` (`ltr` or `rtl`, absent means `ltr`) in the project and in the container; the container can fix its own and, without that, follows the project (the canvas and the floating ones always follow the project). The effective direction reaches the drawing, Arrange and the position of the new child, and changing a container's only re-instantiates its markings. No marking pixel changes; the proposal carries the field in `formatVersion` 8.
- **Reading direction in the app.** The **Direction** field in the container Details ("Follows the project", "Left to right" or "Right to left"), the **Reading direction** section in the project Settings, the Diagnostics section (only with `rtl`, listing the containers that fix a different direction) and the proposal review, which shows the change as the "Direction" field.
- **Direction in the MCP.** `direction` (`ltr`/`rtl`) in `update_project`, `add_container` and `update_container` (`null` goes back to following the project); `get_project`, `get_container` and `get_marking` return the fixed and the effective one; `render_marking` and `render_container` gain `direction` to fix it on the image; `get_code_hints` returns `direction` (the container's effective one); the proposal with a direction writes `formatVersion` 8; `docs/AGENT-GUIDE.md` says how Compose and web apply it; R27.11 covers the container's direction in the app and in the MCP.
- **Viewer and rich text in `rtl`.** The `screen.direction` of the source (or the viewer's `direction` option) reaches the inside of each component of the screen, not just the boxes; rich text in `rtl` reverses the order of the runs within each line and carries `direction: rtl` to the canvas (the order of the glyphs within a run is still without bidi in the MCP's raster).
- **Anchor between markings (schema v20).** A marking can declare another one in the same space (the same container, or both on the canvas) as its anchor: `anchor` with the side (`top`, `bottom`, `start` or `end`, which follow the direction), the alignment (`start`, `center` or `end`) and the offset (the distance from the edge and the offset along the side). It is a relation alongside belonging, like the arrow: the parent is still decided only by geometry. An anchor that does not exist, the marking itself, another space and a cycle are refused (`anchor-missing`, `anchor-self`, `anchor-other-space`, `anchor-cycle`); a project without anchors is written as before, and deleting the anchor leaves the anchored ones without an anchor.
- **Moving the anchor takes the anchored ones along.** Moving the anchor (drag, `update_marking` with `move` in the MCP, multi-selection) moves along the markings anchored to it, even locked ones, and their descendants, in a single undo entry; resizing the anchor moves no one.
- **Snapping to the anchor.** An action computes the rectangle from the side, the alignment and the offset, keeps the size, clamps it inside the container (with a notice) and states the before and the after, or that nothing changed; a locked marking and a marking without an anchor are refused (`locked`, `not-anchored`). `create_marking` with an anchor and no rectangle is already born at the measured size, snapped.
- **The component root says how to snap** (`formatVersion` 4): `anchor` with `side`, `align` and `offset` (in units, converted by the container's density) applies when the marking does not say them.
- **The drawing reads the anchor** (`formatVersion` 4): the fields `$anchor.side`, `$anchor.x`, `$anchor.y`, `$anchor.width` and `$anchor.height` (side, center and size of the anchor in units; empty without an anchor) hold wherever a field holds, to draw, for example, a tooltip's arrow under the anchor's center. Moving the anchor redraws only the markings anchored to it.
- **Anchor in the MCP.** `create_marking` and `update_marking` accept `anchor` (`null` removes the anchor); the `snap_to_anchor` op (of the batch; there is no tool of its own) snaps an already anchored marking, in `plan_changes`, in the batch and in a proposal; **the proposal that writes `anchor` goes up to `formatVersion` 9** (the one that only snaps, with `snap_to_anchor`, does not go up); `get_marking` always returns `anchor` (`null` without an anchor) and `anchoredBy`; a nonexistent `to` comes out as `not-found` (the review shows the "Anchor" field and the snap like the usual Position); `get_marking` returns `anchor` and `anchoredBy`; `get_code_hints` returns `anchor` (the anchor, the side and the alignment) for the agent to write the tooltip, badge and menu wrapper from the notes of the `code`. Errors: `anchor-missing`, `anchor-self`, `anchor-cycle`, `anchor-other-space`, `not-anchored` and `locked`.
- **Agent guide and `spec-author` skill.** The guide brings the "tooltip, badge and menu" pattern (create with `anchor` and without `rect`; moving the anchor takes the anchored ones along; after changing the size, `snap_to_anchor`), the new errors and the list of what only holds in `formatVersion` 4; the skill guides the `anchor` at the root of the tooltip, the badge and the menu and the `$anchor.*`. Scripts R12 (anchor through the MCP), R13 (the review) and R25 (the test tooltip on the four sides) cover the part.
- **Anchor in the app.** The **Anchor** section in the marking Details (the one that draws a component, or that already carries an anchor): **Anchored to** lists the markings of the same container (or of the canvas) with "None"; **Side**, **Alignment** and **Offset** (distance from the edge and offset along the side, in container pixels) each write in an undo entry without moving anything, and the **Snap to anchor** button puts the marking next to the anchor, in a single entry. **Arrange** (button, canvas menu and `Alt+Shift+A`) on a marking with an anchor also snaps, after arranging or fitting to content, in the same undo entry. On the canvas, the selected or hovered anchored marking shows a **thin dashed line** to the anchor, which follows the drag. Dragging the anchor takes the anchored ones along (the preview and the end, in a single entry). In the **Palette**, with a marking selected, a type whose component declares `anchor` at the root is born anchored and snapped to it ("Add anchored to X"); the other types follow the usual rule. Script R9 gains steps R9.32 to R9.36.
