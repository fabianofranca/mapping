Mapping Sans and Mapping Mono (mcp/fonts/*.ttf)

They are subsets (Latin: ASCII, Latin-1, Latin Extended-A and common punctuation), without hinting,
of the Liberation Sans and Liberation Mono 2.x fonts, renamed as the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 requires for modified versions (the reserved name "Liberation" is not used).
