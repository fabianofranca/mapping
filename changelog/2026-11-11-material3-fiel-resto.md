# Faithful Material 3, the rest — 2026-11-11 (épico `epic/material3-fiel-resto`, PR #1513, MCP 0.34.0, schema v18)

## Adicionado

- Component format 4: `font.align: "justify"` justifies the text to the width of the box, with the last line of each paragraph on the left (Compose's `TextAlign.Justify`); the app, the viewer and the MCP PNG wrap the same way. Rich text (`runs`) still draws on the left.
- Component format 4: `over` in the color `$token` (`{ "$token": "on-surface", "alpha": 0.38, "over": "surface" }`) composites the color over another and delivers it opaque over an opaque base (Compose's `compositeOver`; a translucent base passes its alpha on); `over` is the name of a color token or a literal color, and the library refuses the unknown token.
- Component format 4: the `image` node gains the `fit` values `fill-width`, `fill-height`, `scale-down` and `none` (Compose's `FillWidth`, `FillHeight`, `Inside` and `None`), in the app and in the viewer; the MCP PNG keeps drawing the image's placeholder.
- Component format 4: `shadow` accepts a list of 1 to 4 shadows (the first stays on top, like CSS's `box-shadow`) and gains `spread` (px, literal or `$token`, negative shrinks; the radii grow along). The app, the viewer and the MCP PNG draw all of them; the warning "only the first outer shadow" no longer exists, and the root's enlarged crop counts the `spread` and all the shadows.
- Component format 4: `stroke.gap` (`{ "node": "<name>", "padding": 4 }`, in `frame` and `rect`) opens a gap with rounded corners in the outline where the box of the named node crosses it, like the outline of the `OutlinedTextField` under the label; in the app, in the viewer and in the MCP PNG.
- Component format 4: `provide` on the `slot` and `$provided` on the child (inherited color, Compose's `CompositionLocal`): the container offers a value (the content color, for example) resolved in it, and the text, the icon or the icon button inside it reads it with `default` (only in a color position); the closest ancestor that offers the name wins, and with no ancestor the `default` applies. The app, the viewer (screens) and the MCP PNG inherit, and changing the parent's field redraws only the child that reads it. It is drawing only: the `code` does not change.
- In a `formatVersion` 1 to 3 each new key is the error `só é permitido com formatVersion 4`, at its path. The `spec-author` skill, `docs/COMPONENT-FORMAT.md`, `docs/component.schema.json` and `docs/AGENT-GUIDE.md` carry the keys and the messages.
- Material 3 library (Compose), new optional fields (the update from version 6 to 7 remains additive, without loss): `today` on the `date-picker` (the day of the month that marks "today": outline of 1 and number in `primary`; the chosen day takes precedence, and with `month` and `year` a day the month does not have draws nothing), `avatar` on the `input` `chip` (a circular image of 24 at the start, in place of the leading icon), `color`, `insetStart` and `insetEnd` on the `divider` (color role and inset per end; the `inset` applies when both are empty), `trackColor` and `strokeCap` (`Round`, `Butt`, `Square`) on the `progress-indicator`, and the `contentScale` values `FillWidth`, `FillHeight`, `Inside` and `None` of the `image`, which map to the new `fit` values. The `code` carries the parameter when the field is filled.

## Corrigido

- MCP PNG: the text `font.align: "justify"` with the embedded font is now justified as in the app (the line that does not end the paragraph goes to the width of the box); the frame with `clip` and corner radius clips by the shape (the circle of the time-picker handle no longer leaks outside the rectangle); the `plan_changes` summary of `add_container` with a file cites the container's name.
- Component format 4: `stroke.gap.node` inside a `repeat` or citing its own node is now refused in validation (before, it passed and did not open the gap).
- Catalog: the **Code** section renders the inline Markdown of the notes (without literal backticks) and shows `key → value` in the parameters.

## Alterado

- Material 3 library (Compose), version 7 (additive update): adopts the new engine — the text, the icon and the icon button inherit the content color of the `surface` and of the `scaffold` (`on-primary` over `primary`, like `LocalContentColor`); the types with shadow draw the two shadows of each elevation level (point and ambient, with `spread`); the disabled `switch` and `slider` come out in an opaque color, without stacking transparency; the outlined `text-field` and `secure-text-field` open the gap over the label with the rounded corners; the `text` with `textAlign = Justify` draws justified; and the `time-picker` shows, in `on-primary`, the part of the numbers that the handle covers.
- The `dist/index.html` ceiling went up 16 KiB, from 1,966,080 to 1,982,464 bytes, by the budget rule.
