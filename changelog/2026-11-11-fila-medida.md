# Measured and cheaper queue — 2026-11-11 (epic `epic/fila-medida`, PR #1546, MCP 0.34.2 unchanged, schema v18)

## Adicionado

- Cost and effort of the queue sessions in the panel (`painel.py` and skills `implementar-fila` and `detalhar-backlog`): `session … kind=<tipo> [label=]` and `cost` store in `meta.sessions` the cost, the context, the model and the effort of each session (from `get_session`), `costs [--desde <ISO>]` prints the total, the totals by kind (with median) and by stage, `show` gains the `custo:` line and the page, the "Custo da fila" card. The children are born with the `effort` from the table (phase and tester) or `medium` (fix), checked by `effort_level`; `docs/FLOW.md`, `docs/PROCESS.md` and `detalhar-backlog` stop saying that `create_session` only accepts the model.
- Supervisor requests and rules in force in the panel (`painel.py` and skill `implementar-fila`): `sup <pasta> SUP-<n> [tipo=A|B] [pedido=] estado=<estado> [evidencia=]` writes to `meta.requests` (with the history of each state), `requests` prints the table and computes `atrasado` (`enviado` or `aprovado` for more than 90 min); `rule <pasta> add "<texto>" origem=<dono|SUP-n> [saida=]` and `rule <pasta> end R<k> [motivo=]` store in `meta.rules` the owner's fixed rules and the supervisor's, which `show` prints in full (`regras em vigor:`) and which stop existing only in the log. `show` gains `pedidos do supervisor:`, the page gets the cards "Pedidos do supervisor" and "Regras em vigor", and the `SKILL.md` gets the section "Pedidos do supervisor" (closed list of type A, type B asked to the owner, states and when the supervisor is woken up).
- Hot swap on the existing panel and optional migration (`painel.py` and skill `implementar-fila`): `upgrade <pasta>` (idempotent) replaces the hold "ordem do dono (só com a fila vazia)" with `exclusive=sim` and records in the `log` what changed; `import <pasta-nova> <pasta-antiga>` brings to a new panel the order, the `hold`s, the sheets, the rows that wait or are in progress (with `child`, `wait`, `ciWait` and `sessions`), the finished ones in `meta.merged` and the rules in force, without costs, requests, `roles`, context, reserve, `paralelo` or log. The skill does the `upgrade`, the page swap and the `role` at each resumption ("Na sessão nova") and gains the optional argument `importar=<url do painel antigo>`, which redoes the sheet of all the imported rows. `tests/tooling/queueImport.test.ts` checks the old panel and the migration.
- Skill `supervisionar-fila` (phase FM8): queue supervision in an Opus `high` session outside it, with the type A requests sent directly to the coordinator (`SUP-<n>`), the type B ones explained to the owner, the rules for writing and for reading the sources, the automatic session swap at ~25% of context (handoff in the "Supervisão da fila" artifact) and the on-demand cost analysis (`custo` and `varredura`, with `custo.py`, which aggregates a JSONL of `get_session` by group of sessions). `docs/FLOW.md` gains `## Supervisão da fila`, and the `supervisionar-fila` trigger enters `scripts/ci/riskTriggers.mjs` and the "Esforço do detalhamento" of `detalhar-backlog`. `tests/tooling/supervisorSkill.test.ts` checks the text and `custo.py`.

## Alterado

- Coordinator swap at 35% and one active session per role (`painel.py`, skill `implementar-fila` and `docs/FLOW.md`): `CONTEXT_LIMIT` drops from 60 to 35; `role <pasta> <coordenador|supervisor> <id> [link=]` records in `meta.roles` the current session of each role (with `n`) and `roles` prints them; the successor titles itself "Fila — coordenador <n>", archives the predecessor (`archive_session`, now in `permissions.allow`) and checks the state in `get_session`; the `role` keeps the `previous`, the old one records the successor when creating it, only ids registered in the panel are archived, and the session that stopped being the registered one does not act, it only forwards to the current one.
- Queue panel (`painel.py`): `apply` writes the commands of a cycle at once (all or nothing), `meta.unpublished` and the `não publicado` line of `show` say what is left to publish, and `published` and `touch` give the real time of publication. The `implementar-fila` skill now publishes at every turn that wrote, even with the cycle's subagent running.
- Complete sheets, exclusive stage, "Drenar" and sections in the Paralelo (`painel.py` and skills `implementar-fila` and `detalhar-backlog`): `dep`, `toca` or `fases` at `?` give `ficha incompleta (<campos>)` and enter `fichas a refazer:` of `show`, instead of silently holding the queue; `row '#n' exclusive=sim` makes the stage start only with the queue empty and holds the others while it runs (in place of the hold "ordem do dono (só com a fila vazia)"); "Drenar" pauses and, with no stage in progress, announces "Fila drenada" and stops; and `docs/FLOW.md`, `docs/PROCESS.md` and the `SKILL.md` files are compared by `##` section (`painel.py slug` prints the anchor), like `docs/TESTING.md` by script.

- Restricted retest, fix that reproduces the finding and editing subagents at `medium` (`docs/FLOW.md`, `docs/PROCESS.md` and skill `implementar-fila`): the retest asks only for the steps of the findings (and those that the fixes affect), not the whole script; the fix phase reproduces each finding before touching the code, checks it again afterwards and brings the "before" and the "after" in the notice (a finding not reproduced does not count as fixed); the subagents that edit run at `medium` (`high` only in review or checking) and the one that goes past ~200k of context is swapped. `tests/tooling/queuePhaseRules.test.ts` checks the text.

## Corrigido

- `painel.py published` no longer clears what is left to publish: only `published --done`, after the successful `batch`, does. A session that dies between the `published` and the end of the send leaves `show` indicating `não publicado`, and the next session or the hourly check republishes. The test `viewKeepCenter.spec.ts` stopped being flaky (the view reading now waits for the frames to settle).
- At closing, the epic's `/code-review high` review led to three fixes in `painel.py`: `cost` stores the normalized issue, `costs --desde` counts the session without `created` by the time of the cost, and an `upgrade` without change inside `apply` does not print `alterados:` twice; `apply` builds the parser only once.
