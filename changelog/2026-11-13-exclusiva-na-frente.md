# Exclusive in front drains the queue — 2026-11-13 (épico `epic/exclusiva-na-frente`, PR #1839, MCP 0.39.1 unchanged, schema v19)

## Alterado

- **An exclusive in front of the order drains the queue (for whoever drives the flow, #1813):** the exclusive stage (`exclusive=sim`) that is the first candidate by the panel's order (without `hold`, with a complete record and the dependencies merged, and which does not start in the same calculation) now makes the queue stop starting new stages: each candidate after it gets `segurada para a exclusiva #N` (state `exclusiva` in the graph, no new arrow), the ones in progress finish and the exclusive starts when the last one is merged. `show` brings the line `drenando para a exclusiva: #N` (or `nenhuma`) right after `começar agora:`. A common stage in front that waits for a file, schema or slot is the first one and there is no draining; `hold`, an incomplete record and an open dependency do not count. The graph puts the exclusive in front alone in a wave before the ones it holds. The new `ready` enters right after the exclusive that drains, except by the owner's order or a `bug` with `severity: high`. The rule of the exclusive already started and the owner's "Pause" and "Drain" stay as they were.
