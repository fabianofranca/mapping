# Expressions that calculate in format 4 — 2026-11-10 (epic `epic/expressoes-formato-4`, PR #1487, MCP 0.31.0, schema v18)

## Adicionado

- `formatVersion` 4 components do math in expressions: `$add`, `$sub`, `$mul`, `$div`, `$mod` (remainder with the divisor's sign), `$round`, `$floor`, `$ceil`, `$abs`, `$min`, `$max`, `$clamp` and `$sin`/`$cos` (in degrees), where a number is valid and inside `$concat`. An empty operand, non-numeric text or division by zero make the operation produce no value (the place's default applies).
- Comparisons `$gt`, `$gte`, `$lt` and `$lte` in conditions (`$if`, `visible`, `$and`, `$or`, `$not`); an operand with no value gives false.
- Number in text: a non-integer number in `$concat` comes out with up to 6 decimal places, without trailing zeros (`0.1 + 0.2` becomes `0.3`).
- The validator checks literal operand, arity and version (`só é permitido com formatVersion 4` in versions 1 to 3); `docs/component.schema.json`, `docs/COMPONENT-FORMAT.md`, the `spec-author` skill and `docs/AGENT-GUIDE.md` describe the new keys.
- `$rows` (the number of rows of a `table` field) and `$size` (the width or height of the instance, which follows the marking's resize) in number expressions, and the `repeat` by count: `count` in place of `field`, with `{ "$field": "$index" }` for the row index (also in `repeat` by `field`) and the ceiling of 1000 rows (warning `repeat limitado a 1000 linhas: <nó>`).
- Anchors by expression: `left`, `right`, `top` and `bottom` accept a number expression and `centerX` and `centerY` a condition; one that gives no number counts as an absent anchor. All only in the component's `formatVersion` 4 (`só é permitido com formatVersion 4` in the earlier ones).
- `rotation` on any node (except the root) of the `formatVersion` 4 component: rotates the node in degrees, clockwise, around the box's center, in drawing only. The children and the clip of a frame rotate along; hover follows the rotated shape; layout, `hug` and Arrange do not change. The app, the viewer and the MCP PNG (`render_marking`, `render_container`, `get_marking_image`) draw the same, and the rotated clip too. In a `formatVersion` below 4: `só é permitido com formatVersion 4`; on the root: `não vale na raiz`.
- Script R25.75 to R25.78: the bar by `$size`/`$mul` and the rectangle rotated 45° in the MCP PNG and in the app.

## Alterado

- Ceiling of `dist/index.html` went up 16 KiB, from 1949696 to 1966080 bytes, by the budget rule.
- `material3-compose` library at version 6 (additive update): the `date-picker` and `time-picker` draw by calculation, without enumerated tables, and the files drop from 78 kB and 53 kB to 44 kB and 31 kB. The `date-picker` gains the optional fields `month` and `year`: with both, the grid is that of the month (the number of days with the leap year, the weekday of day 1 and 4, 5 or 6 rows); without either, the grid of today stays (31 days starting on Wednesday). The `time-picker` places the handle, the track and the dial numbers by sine and cosine, and the PM and the leading zero by calculation. Both gain the `interaction` field (`none`, `hovered`, `focused` and `pressed`) on the chosen item (the chosen day, the start day of the range or day 1; the hour or the minute field) and new examples (February 2028, 28- and 30-day months, the range that crosses weeks, 10:37 and 22:05, one per state). The scene of the earlier examples stays the same to within 0.5 px.
- A specialization type's example (`examples[].values`) accepts, in a `table` field, the list of rows in the format of the component's `preview` (column → text, number or `null` objects, each cell checked by the column's type; `_id` and a key that is not a column are an error; the `ref` column only accepts `null`; the empty list is valid), without raising the specialization's `formatVersion` (it stays at 15). An app or MCP prior to the epic rejects the new library. The four new errors come out translated (pt-BR and en-US) in the library's problem list and in the `.json` file's; the MCP's `validate_library` brings only the `message` in Portuguese, without `code`. The Catalog gives `_id` to the example's rows, the drawing test draws them and `get_component` returns them as they are in the file; an example that does not bring the field still has no rows.
- `material3-compose` library (stays at version 6): every `values` example of `tabs`, `navigation-bar`, `short-navigation-bar`, `navigation-rail`, `navigation-drawer`, `segmented-button`, `dropdown-menu`, `bottom-sheet` and `carousel` brings the type's `table` field with the rows of the component's `preview` (in the `dropdown-menu`, the examples "Campo exposto aberto" and "Com foco" keep the first row, because with more the drawing test warns that the content does not fit). Script R10.13 (`docs/TESTING.md`).
- `material3-compose` library (stays at version 6, at +1 of the epic over `main`): the `slider` now has numeric `value`, `valueStart` and `valueEnd` (0 to 1, before an `enum` in tenths), with the position by fraction and the value rounded to the `steps` step (0.37 with `steps` 4 draws the handle at 0.4), the `steps` marks exact for any number, and the file drops from 93 kB to 25 kB. The library update brings `migrations` (`fill-field` of the three fields): slider values that were stored as text (`"0.3"`) go back to the default (0.5, 0.2 and 0.8) and appear as a loss in "Migration steps" (owner's decision: no project uses the slider yet).
- `material3-compose` library: the `progress-indicator` (exact arc and width for any `progress` from 0 to 1, from 23 kB to 10 kB), the `carousel` (large, medium and small items by the marking's width, in Compose's strategy; the centered-hero follows the width and the `itemSpacing`), the `short-navigation-bar` (the `Centered` margin of 20%, 15%, 10% and 5% with 3 to 6 items, and the item that grows up to `width/items`) and the `pull-to-refresh` (the indicator's arc and arrow by `rotation`) draw by calculation, without enumerated tables.
- Scripts R17.18 to R17.21 (slider 0.37 with `steps` 4, February 2028, 6-row month, `time-picker` at 10:37 and `focused` on each selector) and R3.18 (slider migration); R17 moved from block 1 to block 5 of the full round, in exchange for R14.
