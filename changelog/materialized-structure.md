# Stored structure for materialized markings — [Não lançado] (épico `epic/materialized-structure`, PR #3145, MCP 0.74.0, schema v27)

## Adicionado

- The **stored structure of a materialized marking** (phase MS1 of #3087, schema v27): a marking whose own annotation has a type that declares a `component` keeps its `parentId` as stored, and the new optional `markings[].slot` (written right after `parentId`, only when set) names the slot of its parent that holds it. The settle only drops an invalid stored parent (missing, itself, another space, floating, decorative or in a cycle) to `null`; a materialized marking is never a parent by geometry, and an observation marking keeps the geometry rule among observation markings only (`src/model/nature.ts`, `settleStructure.ts`).
- A materialized child lives in its parent's space and travels with it; the clipped settle still equals the full one, proved with materialized markings, stored parents and nature changes among the operations (#3137).
- The invariant `slot-without-parent` and its repair `slot-cleared`; `rect-outside-parent` is not reported for a materialized child.
- `addComponentMarking` stores the `parentId` it receives, so a component created inside another keeps its parent now that the geometry no longer picks it.
- **The operations of the stored structure** (phase MS2 of #3087, `src/model/structure.ts`): `setMarkingParent` sets the parent of a materialized marking (a slot of a materialized marking, an observation marking or none; `parent-by-geometry` for an observation marking, `slot-missing` and `invalid-parent` for a bad target, new texts in both locales and in the MCP's error record), taking the marking and its stored subtree into the parent's space with the units kept.
- **The creation rule**: a materialized marking created without a parent, or one that becomes materialized (an annotation with a component type is added, or a library update gives its type a component), takes once the parent the geometry gives, of either nature, and its slot when the catalog is at hand; one that becomes an observation marking loses its `slot` and takes its parent from the geometry (`addTypedAnnotation`, `removeAnnotation`, `convertAnnotationToFree`, `updateSpecialization` and `removeSpecialization` call `applyNatureChanges` and settle).
- **Moving carries the stored subtree** in every space, the canvas included (`moveMarking`, `moveItems`, `nudgeItems`, Details' x and y); an observation marking's geometric children keep the rule of the space, and resizing moves no child. **Wrap** makes the new observation marking the stored parent of the selected materialized markings (a child of a materialized slot fails with `invalid-parent`). `duplicateMarking` and `pasteMarking` copy a marking with its subtree and annotations and keep the parent and the slot, or apply the creation rule when the paste lands in another space.

## Alterado

- In progress: stored structure (parent, slot, order) for materialized markings (#3087).
- `addComponentMarking` with a `parentId` no longer needs the rectangle inside the parent (`rect-outside-parent` is not reported for a materialized child), and writes the slot when the parent has several.
