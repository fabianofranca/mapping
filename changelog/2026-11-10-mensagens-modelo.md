# Model messages in the app's language — 2026-11-10 (epic `epic/mensagens-modelo`, PR #1512, MCP 0.33.1, schema v18)

## Corrigido

- With the app in English (US), the problems that the library validator builds (package window in **Specializations**, Diagnostics and Catalog) come out in English, such as "file not referenced by the specialization", "empty document" and "file not found: …". In Portuguese the text is the same as before, and the output of `validate_library` and `plan_library_update` does not change. The drawing warnings inside the list keep the engine's text until the drawing engine phase.
- With the app in English (US), part of the specialization errors (fields, suggestions, `code`, examples, where it applies, allowed children, documents, roles, groups, types and layers; the rest comes in the next phases) and the default zod texts (invalid type, unknown property, invalid value) come out in English in the library's problem list; in Portuguese, the default zod texts come out translated. The `message`, the `errors` and the output of `validate_specialization` do not change.
- With the app in English (US), the rest of the specialization errors (repositories, platforms, containers, sources, migrations, external sources, `allowedChildren`, `accepts`, `docRepos`, tokens and `icons`), its warnings and the breaks without migration of the refused update come out in English, in the library list, in the refused standalone specialization and in the update dialog. In Portuguese the text is the same as before, and the output of `validate_specialization`, `validate_library` and `plan_library_update` does not change.
- With the app in English (US), the errors and warnings of the component (the `.json` file of each type), of the icon set and of the sources declaration come out in English in the library's problem list, in the refused standalone specialization and in the notice of the component that did not load (Catalog and Diagnostics). In Portuguese the text is the same as before, and the output of `validate_library`, `validate_specialization` and `plan_library_update` does not change.
- With the app in English (US), the drawing engine warnings (field not provided, content larger than the box, `fill` inside `hug`, unknown icon, type without a component, invalid node and the other ones about sources and measurement) come out in English in the Catalog, in the Playground, in Diagnostics and in the drawing proof of the problem list. In Portuguese the text is the same as before, and the text `warnings`, the MCP and the viewer API do not change.
- With the app in English (US), the errors that prevent the translation of an example screen of the sources (source not declared, screen width or height missing) and the "invalid JSON" of a standalone specialization also come out in English. In Portuguese the text is the same as before, and the model's text `error` does not change. A guard test (`tests/i18n/modelMessageCoverage.test.ts`) runs invalid documents through the validator, the parsers and the engine and fails if any problem or warning comes out without a code.
