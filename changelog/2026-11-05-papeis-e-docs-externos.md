# Roles and external documents — 2026-11-05 (épico `epic/papeis-e-docs-externos`, PR #568, MCP 0.18.0, schema v14)

## Adicionado

- Specialization `formatVersion` 11, with three optional features (11 is additive: versions 1 to 10 are still accepted, and each new key in an earlier version is the error `only allowed with formatVersion 11`, at the exact path):
  - **Children by layer and group:** an `allowedChildren` item can be a `{ layer, groups? }` clause that covers a whole layer or only its groups, instead of listing children one by one; the layer declares the groups (`layers[].groups`) and the type says which ones it belongs to (`annotationTypes[].groups`). `requiresOwner` is now checked against the resolved set of children, and validation warns when an owner lists 20 ids or more one by one and when a declared group has no type.
  - **Roles in the link:** `childRoles` (and `childRolesLabel`, the name of the selector) on the owner type declares the points where a child links to it, without creating a type per trigger or a field on the child.
  - **Document in an external repository:** `docRepos` at the root declares the document repositories and the type's `doc` can be `{ repo, path }`, an `.md` file in one of them, instead of a file in the package. The external document does not count as a package file.
- The JSON Schema (`docs/spec.schema.json`), the Help → Specializations page (pt-BR and en-US), `docs/SPEC-FORMAT.md` and the `spec-author` skill describe `formatVersion` 11, with the three new sections, the validation rules and the anti-patterns (trigger in the type id, `trigger` field in every event and pairwise `allowedChildren`).
- **The link role in the project:** the linked annotation stores in `parentRole` the role it holds in the owner (the `id` of a `childRoles` of the owner's type, or none). Creating the annotation already linked and with a role, changing the role and changing the owner are each one undo entry; an owner that does not offer the current role clears the role in the same entry, and so does removing the owner.
- **Role pending items** in the Incomplete window and in the "Incomplete" badge: the child without a role in an owner that has roles (`missing-role`) and the child with a role that the owner does not offer (`unknown-role`). They block nothing; a free owner or one of an unknown type does not generate a role pending item.
- **Clone of the documents in the project:** `docRepos` stores the `localPath` of the clone of each document repository declared by the specialization (saving and removing are undoable actions).
- Proposals carry the annotation's `parentRole` and the `docRepo` entity, compared, decided and applied like the other fields; the proposal format stays at 4.
- **Type picker with search and groups:** the type list of "+ Annotation" gets a search field at the top ("Search among N types", with focus on open; filters by name, id and description, ignoring case and accents) and, in layers that declare groups, headers per group in the layer's order, with ungrouped types under "Other" at the end. With no result, the list says "No type matching \"<text>\"".
- **The role in the same step as the owner choice:** when creating a type that requires an owner, if the owner has roles the list offers one option per owner and role ("Button Continuar › Clique"), the same two clicks as before; with an owner without roles, the step does not change.
- **Link by role on the owner and on the child:** on an owner that declares `childRoles`, "Linked" shows one row per role (with the children, "none" when empty and a "+ Link…" per row), and children without a role, or with a role the owner does not offer, go in a last row "No <label>" with the pending-item mark. On the child's card, next to "Linked to", a role selector (with the `childRolesLabel` label) saves the role; switching to an owner that does not offer the role clears it in the same undo entry. The List shows the role ("↳ of Button Continuar (Clique)") and proposal review shows the role change and the document repository change.
- **Document of a type in an external repository, on the component page:** the Document section reads the `.md` from the repository's clone folder, which the person connects **once per machine** ("Connect the <repo> folder…"; the app only reads, and the folder is valid only in this browser and on this machine, and never goes in `mapping.json`, in the zip or in the commit). With the folder connected the document shows up, with a discreet "Open in repository" link. Without permission (the browser asked again) the section offers "Allow access to <repo>"; without the file in the folder, it says "<path> is not in <repo>" and offers "Change folder…". In a browser without a folder picker, or without `urlTemplate` in the specialization, only the link or `<repo>: <path>` appears.
- **Documents section in the Repositories dialog:** each document repository declared by the specialization gets the "Local path of the root" field, which stores the clone's `localPath` and is what the MCP uses to read the document.
- **MCP 0.18.0:** the `role` (the `id` of a `childRoles` of the owner) in `create_annotation` and `update_annotation` (`null` clears; `unknown-role` and `role-without-owner` are batch errors, and a missing role becomes `missing-role` in the plan's `issues`, without failing); `parentRole` and `roleName` on the child annotation, `linkedByRole` (`label`, `roles` and `unassigned`) on the owner with roles and `parentRole` in the linked tree of `get_marking` and in `get_code_hints`; `set_doc_repo` and `remove_doc_repo` in the batch (with `changes.docRepos`) and `docRepos` in `get_project`; the external document in `get_component` (with the clone configured, `doc` is the text of the `.md` and `docExternal` carries `repo`, `path`, `url` and `localFile`; without a clone, without the file or outside the working directory, `doc` is `null` and the link and a `hint` come along; a file over 1 MiB is not read); and `docClones` in `validate_library` (a document missing from the clone is a warning, and one warning per repository without a clone counts the documents not checked). `plan_library_update` checks the external document by the project's `localPath`, and `validate_specialization` accepts `formatVersion` 11.

## Alterado

- **Schema v14:** `mapping.json` gets `annotations[].parentRole` and `docRepos`. The v13 → v14 migration is automatic, with the original backed up in `backups/` and the banner at the top: every existing link ends up without a role and `docRepos` starts empty. A role without an owner or an empty one refuses to open the file, with the message. An earlier app opens the v14 project read-only, and an earlier MCP does not write to it.
- **Creating from the owner always opens the "Link" dialog:** the direct per-type buttons ("+ onClick") no longer exist. "+ Link…" opens the type list (with search and groups) restricted to what the owner accepts, also when there is only one type, with the title "Link to <owner>" or, in a role, "Link to <owner> › <role>"; the annotation is born in the type's layer, linked and with the role, in one undo entry.
- **The "Code repositories" dialog became "Repositories"**, with the subsections "Code" (the usual code repositories) and "Documents" (those from `docRepos`).
- **The SDUI example teaches roles and groups:** `examples/specs/sdui.json` moves to `formatVersion` 11 (version 4). The `onClick`, `onHold` and `onChange` events go away and `action` (Action), `feedback` (Feedback) and `validation` (Validation) come in, in the Events layer, in groups (Interaction and Data). Button, Input and Image list their children by clause (`allowedChildren`), and the trigger becomes the child's role in the owner (`childRoles`: Click and Press in Button, Change in Input, Click in Image; label "Trigger"). Button carries the `doc` in an external repository (`docRepos`). Updating a project with SDUI v3 goes through the version contract (three `rename-type` steps), and the migrated links end up without a role (`missing-role` pending item) until someone chooses the role.
- **The example project** (`exemplo-completo`) and the guide use the new SDUI, with the events already linked by role and no role pending items; the guide steps stop citing "+ onClick" and move to "+ Link…". Test scripts R1.16 and R1.17 follow the new SDUI, R3.8e checks the v13 → v14 migration, and R19 (roles in the link) and R20 (document in an external repository) join the full round.
