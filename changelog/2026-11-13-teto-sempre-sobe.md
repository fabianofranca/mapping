# Ceiling of dist/index.html that always rises — 2026-11-13 (épico `epic/teto-sempre-sobe`, PR #1821, MCP unchanged, schema v19)

## Alterado

- **Raising the `dist/index.html` ceiling (for whoever drives the flow, #1802):** `node scripts/ci/distBudget.mjs raise` always raises the ceiling, in steps of 16 KiB (`k = ceil((excesso + 2 KiB) / 16 KiB)`, a margin of at least 2 KiB), and never answers "chama o dono". When the raise crosses a limit (excess above 14 KiB, growth above 32 KiB over `main`, or raises since the owner's review above 64 KiB), the script gives the reason on a second line, `bug: …`, and with `--write` the `history` sentence gains ` Acima dos limites: …`, so that the phase opens an owner-review `bug` and goes on without waiting. The numbers of the limits do not change.
- **The phase no longer waits for the owner on the `dist/index.html` ceiling (for whoever drives the flow, #1802):** `docs/FLOW.md` (item 6 of "Para a sessão da fase") and the `implementar-fila` skill ("CI vermelho no PR do épico") now say that `raise` always raises. When it brings the `bug:` line, whoever holds the red PR opens the ceiling `bug` (fixed title "Teto do dist/index.html acima dos limites: …", `severity: low`, searching by title before opening, one per raise, no `PushNotification`), cites it in the notice, in the changelog line, in the panel `log` and in the comment on #1493; `docs/PROCESS.md` ("Rótulos") allows this case in `bug` and `/detalhar-backlog` leaves it out of the candidates. The closed list of the "Autorização do dono" (item 8) is left with two cases, (a) CI or permission edit and (b) exception to a guard, and the skill gains the transition sentence for the phase of an earlier epic that still asks the ceiling's "chama o dono".
