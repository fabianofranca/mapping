# SVG paint import: gradients and clips in `import_icons` — [Não lançado] (épico `epic/svg-paint-import`, PR #0, MCP 0.62.0, schema v24, unchanged)

## Adicionado

- `import_icons` converts the linear and radial gradients that Figma exports (#2847). A `fill="url(#id)"` on a `linearGradient` or `radialGradient` in `gradientUnits="userSpaceOnUse"`, with any `gradientTransform` (`matrix`, `translate`, `scale`, `rotate`, `skewX`, `skewY`), becomes the icon's gradient `fill`: a linear one writes `from` and `to` (exact under any affine transform), a radial one writes `center` and `radius` (only a circle converts: an elliptical one is an `errors` item that names the gradient and does not stop the other files). Stops keep their `stop-opacity` in the color alpha. The icons file goes up to `formatVersion` 4 when a written icon has a gradient. A gradient on a `stroke`, `href`, `spreadMethod` other than `pad`, a focal point and a path with no width or height under a box gradient are still `errors` items, named.
- `import_icons` converts gradients in `objectBoundingBox`, SVG's default when `gradientUnits` is absent (Sketch and hand-written SVG), with the exact box of the path that uses the gradient (#2847). Fractions and percentages run across that box, the box diagonal keeps its isolines, `gradientTransform` composes after the box, and one gradient on a `g` takes each path's own box. A radial gradient on a box that is not square is an ellipse and goes to `errors`, named.
- `import_icons` converts the `clipPath` that Figma exports for circular icons (#2847). A `clip-path="url(#id)"` on a `g` or on a shape whose `clipPath` holds one shape (`path`, `rect`, `circle`, `ellipse`, `polygon` or `polyline`) becomes the icon's `clip`, with `fillRule` for `clip-rule="evenodd"`; the paint properties of the clip's shape are ignored, Figma's full-`viewBox` wrapper is still dropped and a `clipPath` that nothing references is ignored. The icons file goes up to `formatVersion` 4 when a written icon has a `clip`. A clip that cannot be one for the whole icon (several shapes in one `clipPath`, `objectBoundingBox` units, a `transform` on the `clipPath` or its shape, a clip inside another clip, a clip over only part of the icon) is an `errors` item, named.
- The test fixture `tests/fixtures/svg-icons/paint/brand.svg` is the Figma form of the `brand` icon of `library-gaps`: `import_icons` of it gives the same gradients and clip as the hand-written icon, and the MCP PNG of the `brand-badge` paints the same probes (#2847).

- `tests/mcp/iconImportPaint.test.ts` runs `import_icons` over the `paint/` fixtures end to end (`dryRun`, the real import with `replace`, `validate_library`, the written `fill` and `clip` and the PNG probes). The script `docs/TESTING.md#R39` gains R39.17 to R39.19 (block 6 of the full round, 143 to 146 steps).
