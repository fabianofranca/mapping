# A low-severity-only finding goes to closing without a retest — 2026-11-13 (epic `epic/low-sem-reteste`, PR #1838, MCP 0.39.1 unchanged, schema v19)

## Alterado

- **A finding of low severity only goes from the fix to closing, without a retest (for whoever drives the flow, #1814):** when the findings of a tester round (❌, observations that are defects, and pending defects) are **all low severity**, the fix is made as usual and, once merged into the epic, the stage proceeds to closing with no retest round; the check before and after the fix itself takes its place, including when it changes `src/` (a test seen failing beforehand, or a before-and-after capture for a visual finding). In a mixed round, the retest covers only the high and medium findings; a low finding that the fix does not reproduce gets a retest of its own. The severity of the observation and of the pending defect comes from the scale in section 7 of `docs/TESTING.md`, the higher one when in doubt, and the fix sub-issue records `reteste: sim|não`. `docs/FLOW.md`, `docs/PROCESS.md` and `implementar-fila` state the rule; the manual flow, the owner's standalone round and the five-round limit do not change.
