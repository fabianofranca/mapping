# Owner authorization in the phase chat — 2026-11-11 (épico `epic/autorizacao-na-fase`, PR #1622, MCP 0.34.3 unchanged, schema v18)

## Alterado

- The "Para a sessão da fase" message of `docs/FLOW.md` now sends the owner's authorization (closed list: the "calls the owner" of the `dist/index.html` ceiling, a CI or permission edit that Auto mode denied or that the sub-issue does not foresee, and an exception to a guard) to the phase's own chat: chat link, `PushNotification`, "Esperando o dono" notice to the coordinator and only then the `AskUserQuestion`; after the answer, the phase notifies "O dono respondeu". A stopped subagent, an error and "how to proceed" keep going to the coordinator (sub-item "Decisão de produto").
- Every notice from the phase to the coordinator goes through the `send_message` of `claude-code-remote`, with `session_id` and `status: delivered` checked, never through `SendMessage` (item 7, "Canal").
- The "Orquestrador — passo a passo" does not relay the answer to an owner authorization and reads the question of the stopped phase (`list_events`) before calling the owner.
- The `implementar-fila` skill does not relay the owner's authorization: it marks the stage `esperando dono` only after checking that the reason in the "Esperando o dono" message is on the closed list, answers by rule 1 (`send_message` with `priority: "now"`) whatever is not, goes back to `em andamento` with "O dono respondeu" or the final notice, and the hourly check reads the pending `AskUserQuestion` of the stopped phase (`blocked`) before marking. The "Fim" and the "Drenar" count the `esperando dono` stage with a child as a stage that is still working, and the panel rule with the "#N mesclada" output falls in the same cycle in which #N is merged.
- `painel.py show` gains the line `esperando o dono no chat da fase:`, separate from `esperando o coordenador:` and from `atrasadas:`.

## Corrigido

- The phase that waits for the owner in its chat is now rechecked: `painel.py show` gains `esperando o dono há mais de 1 h:` (with "prazo vencido" from 240 min) and the hourly check rereads the session (`get_session`), recreates the one that died, puts back to `em andamento` the one that was already answered and interrupts the question without an answer in 240 min (`send_message` with `priority: "now"`; the phase records the item in "Defeitos pendentes").
- The phase waiting for the owner is recognized by the mark `dono no chat da fase:` in the `wait` (helper `is_owner_wait`), and not by the `status` `esperando dono`, which also applies to a requested merge and a high-severity finding; the `import` counts these phases as in progress and the "Drenar" and the "Fim" use the same definition.
- In `docs/FLOW.md` the phase prompt no longer says that nobody looks at the session, the phase loads `PushNotification` and `AskUserQuestion` with `ToolSearch`, case (c) of the authorization became an objective list and the undelivered notice gained a fallback ("Aviso não entregue:" comment on the sub-issue, read at the hourly check).
