# Conversation during review — [Não lançado] (épico `epic/conversation-during-review`, PR #2536, MCP no change, schema v18)

## Adicionado

- **Writing in the conversation during the review (CV3, stage #1881):** with a proposal under review, the person replies, resolves and reopens existing conversations in the Conversation window, and nothing is written to `mapping.json` or to the proposal. A new conversation, **Comment**, deleting and a new citation of an item that only exists in the preview still wait for the end of the review. A new message from the agent says "New message from <name>" in a polite live region and never takes the focus. Opening the review (from the card or the Proposals list) shows the proposal's conversation in the window.
- **The "In review" / "Revised" card (CV3):** the proposal card in a conversation says "In review" while its proposal is under review (no button) and "Revised · N to check" after the agent revised it; the card, the review header, the row flag and the conversation line ("linked to the proposal under review") update in the same render.
- **The way back to the review (CV3):** leaving the review (**Exit** or the "Exit the review?" dialog) puts the focus in the composer of the conversation in the window, and a notice at the top of Details ("The proposal "<title>" is still open.") offers **Review** to go back or dismiss.

- **Docs and test scripts (CV4, stage #1881):** `docs/COMMENT-FORMAT.md` and `docs/AGENT-GUIDE.md` now say that the person can keep writing in an existing conversation while reviewing its proposal, and that a tweak asked there is fixed with `revise_proposal` in the same proposal plus a `post_message`. These two files are release inputs, so the MCP gets a **patch** (the header says "no change" until the epic closing picks the number; no tool, argument, error code or format changes; the number is chosen at the epic closing). R22 was rewritten in place for the **Conversation** window, and the new script **R43 — Conversation during review** runs alone in block 6 of the full round (R41 and R42 were already taken).

## Alterado

- **Budget:** the `dist/index.html` ceiling went up 16 KiB, from 2408448 to 2424832, by the budget rule; above the limits, `bug` #2552 for the owner to review.
- **Conversation window (CV1, stage #1881):** a new tool window stacked under Details in the right column, with its own strip button (icon `conversation`), a splitter (drag to resize, double click to reset) and a stored height (`conversationHeight`, default `size-tw-conversation` = 320 px). It has no `Ctrl+Shift` number, so the Help shortcut list and the guide's table do not change. Its body only shows the empty state for now.
- **One place for conversations (CV2, stage #1881):** every conversation now opens in the Conversation window: the Comments list, a pin, a citation chip, **New**, **Comment**, **Comment here** and sending a new conversation all show it there, and nothing is shown or written inside Details any more. The window has the state badge in its header, an "on <where>" line, the thread and the composer, and it does not follow the selection (the draft stays when the selection changes). The Conversations section of Details is now a list of the item's conversations (a row opens it in the window, **New** opens its composer there). The panel of a conversation without an item (layer, loose point, orphan) is gone: it opens in the window. The identity notice (**Fill in Settings**) appears in the window, in view and focused, on every request.
