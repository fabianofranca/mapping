# Queue early start — 2026-11-20 (épico `epic/queue-early-start`, PR #2386, MCP unchanged, schema v22 unchanged)

## Alterado

- `painel.py` starts a stage early when its only blockers are file conflicts with stages already in their tester, correction or closing: the reason in `vagas:` is `começa cedo (espera #S para testar)`, `começar agora:` prints `#E (cedo: #S)`, and `row` writes the holders in the new `cedo` field. A stage in implementation, a failed or stopped one, one with `cedo` pending, an exclusive one and a fast track never open it; the schema rule, a shared format family or an unknown `formato=` still hold the pair, and a fast-track candidate never starts early.
- The new status `esperando entrega` marks an early stage that finished its phases and waits for its holders' merge before its own tester: it takes no slot, its step reads `espera merge de #S, #T para testar`, and `show` prints `esperando merge para testar:` and `pode sincronizar:` (the holders merged, failed, refused or gone). The queue graph gets the `entrega` state, the `cedo` of each node and an `entrega` arrow from each open holder.
- The ficha gains the field `formato=` (the format families a stage raises); a waiting row without it goes into `fichas a refazer:` without being held. The session kind `sincronização` joins the cost and usage tables, with no signals.
- The coordinator now does three more things in the `implementar-fila` skill (new step 2b of "Per stage"): at the last phase of an early stage it writes `esperando entrega` and frees the slot; at `pode sincronizar:` it creates the sync session `SM<k>` (Sonnet `medium`, message in `docs/FLOW.md` › "For the tester" › "Main before the tester (early start)": `git merge origin/main` on `sync/<name>-sm<k>`, both deliveries kept, a real disagreement as a product decision, the gates, a PR `SM<k> — main com #S`, notice `Sincronização SM<k> pronta`) or, with every holder released, just clears `cedo`; after the notice it clears `cedo` and starts the tester (or the closing). The ficha subagent writes `formato=` after `schema=`, and the "Parallel", "Panel" and "Stage ficha" texts and `docs/FLOW.md` say all of it; `tests/tooling/queueEarlyStartText.test.ts` keeps them in step with `painel.py`'s output.
- The owner sees it on the panel page: the state "waiting for a merge to test" (`entrega`), the tag `cedo · espera #S para testar` on a stage that started early, the arrow kind "delivery", and the "Waiting for the owner or a delivery" tile now counts `esperando entrega` with `esperando dono`. A panel recorded before the stage draws as before. The one-sentence rule is in `docs/FLOW.md` › "For the owner, in the three chats".
