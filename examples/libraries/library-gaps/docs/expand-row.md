# Expand row

A container (300 × 56) whose single slot is a `row` with a gap of 8, padding of 8 and `align: center`.

Its children are Expand items. The row hands the free width to the children that have a root `weight` (see Expand item); a child without one keeps its own width. Arrange does this when the row is arranged or when a child is added, moved or resized.

Annotation type: `expand-row` (Expand row). No fields.
