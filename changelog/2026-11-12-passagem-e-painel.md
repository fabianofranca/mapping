# Context handoff and queue panel — 2026-11-12 (epic `epic/passagem-e-painel`, PR #1758, MCP 0.38.1 unchanged, schema v19)

## Alterado

- Queue panel: the exclusive stage that has started and is not yet merged keeps holding the queue in any state, and the candidates' reason now states the real state (`exclusiva #N falhou`, `exclusiva #N esperando dono`, `exclusiva #N aguardando`; `em andamento` as before). The way out belongs only to the owner: with the owner's order in the queue chat, the coordinator writes `row '#N' exclusive=` and the `log` "exclusiva #N liberada pelo dono" (`painel.py` and "Parallel" of the `implementar-fila` skill).
- Coordinator session handoff: in the new session, right after downloading the panel's three files and before `show`, run `painel.py published <folder> --done`. The published copy carries the `publishing` of the upload that succeeded, and without this step `show` said "não publicado" with nothing pending (`implementar-fila` skill, "Session handoff" and "Panel").
- Phase context handoff: the line `Retomada por contexto (<k> de 5)` lives only in the sub-item "Phase context ceiling" of `docs/FLOW.md` ("Limits" and the `implementar-fila` skill cite it, without copying). The phase or fix PR with the commit `WIP <código>: passagem por contexto` is merged with `squash`, and the red commit does not enter the epic's history. The phase now switches sessions up to 4 times (5 sessions, before 3): when creating the 4th and 5th, the coordinator warns the owner through `PushNotification`, and the fifth does not switch, with the final notice "passou de 300 mil na quinta sessão".
- Tests of the context switch (`tests/tooling/queuePhaseContext.test.ts`): the numbers (maximum of switches, total of sessions, `<k> de N`) and the start of the switch request are compared among item 3 of `docs/FLOW.md`, "Limits" and the `implementar-fila` skill, and the `row`, `session` and `cost` commands of the skill's paragraph run on a temporary panel (one `apply`, phase and fix).
- Squash of the WIP when the coordinator merges the phase: the `implementar-fila` skill tells it to run `git fetch origin <phase branch>` before the `git log` that detects the `WIP` commit, so that the origin ref is neither missing nor stale.
