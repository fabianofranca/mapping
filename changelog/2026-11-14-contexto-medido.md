# Coordinator context measured by the panel — 2026-11-14 (épico `epic/contexto-medido`, PR #1909, MCP unchanged, schema v19)

## Adicionado

- **`painel.py context <pasta> --medir --max <max_tokens>` measures the context and records it with the source (for whoever drives the flow, #1874, CX1):** the command runs `scripts/ci/contextUsage.mjs --max <max_tokens>` (with the session's environment and a 30 s timeout), records the `pct` (above 100 becomes 100), the `contextAt` and the `contextFonte` (`sessao`, or `recente` when the `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID` variable is missing) and prints `medido: pct=<pct> contexto=<contexto> fonte=<fonte>`, the `contexto` that serves for the `ctx` of the session's own cost in the "Troca de sessão". Without a measurement (code 1 of the script) the command fails, without recording, with the reason and the exception path `context <pasta> <pct> --fonte get_session`. It also works inside `apply`. Usage errors (`--medir` without `--max`, `--max` without `--medir`, `--medir` with `<pct>`, neither of the two, `--fonte` with `--medir`) exit with code 2 and nothing recorded.
- **Source of the measurement and warnings on the `contexto:` line of `show` (for whoever drives the flow, #1874, CX1):** the line becomes `contexto: <pct>% (<quando>, <fonte>)`, with `—` on an old record without a source. The hand-typed number (`context <pasta> <pct>`) is still accepted and records the `manual` source; with `--fonte get_session` (the only accepted value) it records `get_session`, the path without a measurement. After the 35% alert, `show` flags the hand-typed measurement (source `manual` or absent) and the stale measurement (more than 70 min), both with "meça com context --medir".

## Alterado

- **The `implementar-fila` skill now measures through the command (for whoever drives the flow, #1874, CX1):** the reserve check, the command list and the description of `show` in "Painel", the measurement and the `ctx` of the session's own cost in "Troca de sessão" and step 3 of "Na sessão nova" cite `painel.py context <pasta> --medir --max <max_tokens>`, and `<pct> --fonte get_session` when there is no measurement. The new session records its own measured context when it takes over, and no longer a hand-typed number. The 35% ceiling and the "Troca de sessão" do not change.
