# Adjustments from the perspectives round — 2026-11-08 (epic `epic/perspectivas-ajustes`, PR #931, MCP 0.28.3, schema v18)

## Corrigido

- **Deleting a perspective** through **Manage perspectives** no longer leaves the screen without Manage: **Cancel**, `Esc` and **Delete** reopen Manage. After **Cancel**/`Esc` the focus stays on the **Delete** of the same row; after **Delete**, on the **Delete** of the row that took its place (the one below; without it, the one above; without any, on **Close**). Closing Manage still returns the focus to the perspective picker.
- **Arrival after reloading:** the notice "No reference; it went to the arrival area." in Details and the **Arrival area** strip no longer depend on the session in which the root arrived. They are now deduced from the current arrows (the root has a reference when an arrow links one of its members to a member of another root): the root without a reference shows the notice and stays inside the strip, the one with a reference carries only the "Came from X" badge. The `mapping.json` does not change format.
- **Mark legend in the review:** with the legend open and the focus on the **Mark legend** button or on the canvas (including after opening with `L`), `Esc` closes only the legend and the focus stays where it was; the next `Esc` does what it did before (for example, clear the selection). With the focus inside the legend, `Esc`, the X and `L` still work as before.
- **Dragging that touches:** dragging a container (alone or in the selection, in Main and in the perspectives) against another that does not move no longer stops at the pointer's last free step: the image touches the obstacle, with equal edges and no overlap, and slides along it on the other axis. It is still one undo entry per drag; the keyboard arrows stay as they were.
- **Diagnostics:** the **Remote images** list no longer cites an image that came to be shown in the same session (for example, after **Allow these**).
- **Dragging that touches (text):** with a canvas marking in the selection the displacement is an integer, so against a container with a fractional `placement` the image stops without overlapping and less than 1 unit from the obstacle (never on top); without a canvas marking in the selection it still touches exactly. Script R12.10 and appendix A now say this.
