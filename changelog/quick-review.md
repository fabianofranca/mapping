## Adicionado

- Quick review on the canvas: a small proposal linked to the conversation open in the Conversation window can be accepted in one write with one undo entry (Ctrl+Z brings the proposal back undecided) or rejected on this device, which writes nothing and hides it until the agent revises the proposal. This first part adds the eligibility rule (at most 5 markings in one container or on the canvas; creations and changes of markings and annotations; no removals), the quick review state of the open conversation, Accept and the Reject-on-this-device state. The ghost on the canvas, the bar and the card come in the next parts.
