Fonts of the Material 3 (Compose) package
=========================================

The specialization (material3-compose.spec.json) declares Roboto in "fonts":

  - fonts/Roboto-Regular.woff2 (weight 400)
  - fonts/Roboto-Medium.woff2  (weight 500)

The files ship with the package: they are the Latin subset (it includes the Portuguese accents)
of Roboto 5.3.0 from the Fontsource project (@fontsource/roboto), licensed under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1. The license text is in LICENSE-Roboto.txt, next to it.

To switch to another version of Roboto (for example the full .ttf from
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto), copy the files into this folder and adjust the
names in the specialization's "fonts". Without the files, the rendering falls back to the system
font and Diagnostics shows one warning per missing font; the package remains valid.
