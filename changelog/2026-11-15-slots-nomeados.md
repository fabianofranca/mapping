# Named slots with default content — 2026-11-15 (épico `epic/slots-nomeados`, PR #1976, MCP 0.42.0, schema v20)

- A child of a slot hidden by `visible` stays assigned to that slot (bug #1978, option A): it stays in the model and in `get_code_hints`, is not drawn (nor its descendants), keeps its outline and stays selectable, and gets the pending item `slot-hidden` ("the slot {slot} of {owner} is hidden") in the Incomplete window, the list, `get_marking`, `list_markings` and the `issues.new` of `plan_changes` and `propose_changes`. A slot hidden by `visible` is still a candidate with the box it would have if visible (a second `instantiate` pass, `revealSlots`, only when the marking has children and a slot is missing from the probe); a visible slot wins an equal intersection unless the hidden slot's box is smaller. `get_marking` lists the hidden slot with `hidden: true`. Arrange and "add inside" no longer move its child into another slot; `docs/COMPONENT-FORMAT.md` and `docs/MCP.md` say so.

## Adicionado

- Component format (`formatVersion` 4): the `slot` node gains `id` (pattern `^[a-z][a-zA-Z0-9_-]*$`, so it can equal the parameter of a platform, like `floatingActionButton`) and `fallback`, the default content: a `frame` drawn while no child marking is in the slot. A component can now have several slots, each one with its own `id`; with a single slot the `id` stays optional and everything works as before. In a `formatVersion` 1 to 3 both keys are `only allowed with formatVersion 4`.
- The box rule: the `visible`, the sizes, the `anchors` and the `layout` of a slot and of its ancestors in the template cannot cite a field that only the `fallback` cites (`slot: the box cannot depend on a field of the default content: <field>`), because the default content is replaced by the child and the box has to be the same with or without it.
- Which slot a child marking is in is derived from the geometry, never written to the project: the greatest intersection with the slot's box, the closest one when there is none, and a tie goes to the template order; a type that a slot accepts wins over a slot that accepts everything (`accepts` and `max` come with the specialization in a later phase). A new child with no geometry goes to the requested slot, else to the first one that accepts its type and is not full.
- `docs/COMPONENT-FORMAT.md` ("Several slots and default content"), `docs/component.schema.json`, `docs/AGENT-GUIDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and the `spec-author` skill: the slot `id` and `fallback`, the rule that a code content lambda becomes a slot named by its parameter, and the pitfalls (numbered fields for free content, a slot without `id` in a component with two, a slot box that depends on the default content).
- Several slots and default content in the drawing: the `fallback` of a slot is drawn, and measured in the slot's `hug`, only while the slot is empty, and a `hug` slot that holds children measures them (their box plus the slot's `padding`). It works in the app, in the MCP's image and in the viewer, which hides the `fallback` of the slots that receive children of the screen's tree. The "content larger than the box" warning names the slot (`content larger than the box: slot <id>`).
- Arrange, the layout of a screen without geometry and the position of a new instance work per slot: each child is arranged by the layout of its own slot, a new child goes after the last one of the same slot, and the request can name the slot (`slot`).
- The drawing of each component says its slots, which slot each child is in and which default-content fields are inactive (`slots`, `slotOf`, `inactiveFields`).
- `docs/COMPONENT-FORMAT.md` ("Several slots and default content", "Sizing", "Engine warnings", "`clip` on the frame and the slot", "Known limits") and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`: the probe and the final drawing, the rounded clip and the two differences between the app and the MCP's image (the slot's own fill, which only shows in the PNG, and the truncated label of the `segmented-button`, `S…` in the app and `…` in the PNG).
- Test library `rounded-bar` (`tests/fixtures/slots`): a bar with a radius of 16 and a slot with `clip`.
- Specialization `formatVersion` 17: a type declares the slots of its component in `slots` (`id`, `name`, `accepts` with the same clauses as `children`, and `max` of at least 1), and `code.<platform>.slots` maps a slot id to the parameter that receives it (also in a `variants` case). In a `formatVersion` 16 or lower both keys are `only allowed with formatVersion 17`; the library validation checks them against the component (`slot does not exist in the component: <id>`, `slots: the component has no slot with an id`, `accepts` with a type, layer or group that does not exist) and against the signature sheet (`parameter outside the signature`). `diffSpecs` does not count them as a contract change.

## Corrigido

- A child marking drawn in a slot with `clip` inside a frame with rounded corners is now cut by the rounding of the frame, in the app and in the MCP's image (before, it was cut by the slot's rectangle and the corners stuck out). Without a radius nothing changes.
- A child that is inside its slot no longer reports `component-overflow`: the overflow of a slot with a `hug` axis counts the extent of its children, not their position (the Scaffold and Bar false positives of the Contacts screen, the second pin in `actions`).
- A `hug` slot that grows takes in the children assigned to it, nearest first, so the farthest child no longer strays to the other slot (`actions` next to `floatingActionButton`).
- The children born in a `bottom-app-bar` without `rect` are centered across the bar, and the extended FAB keeps its width (the `floatingActionButton` slot is `hug` and its fallback frame is 56 × 56; the library `version` stays 16).
- `plan_changes` and `propose_changes` list in `issues.new` only what the marking did not already have before the batch, and `before`, `after` and `resolved` count the drawing items too.
- The Catalog drag (`useComponentDrop`) hands the drawings to `addComponent`, like the Palette does.
- The MCP no longer instantiates the drawings once per list row when the project has no valid drawing key, and `loadProject` no longer rereads and rehashes the component files that did not change.
- In `swipe-to-dismiss`, a child in the right-hand strip with `direction` = `EndToStart` and `enableDismissFromEndToStart` off stays in `backgroundContent` (not drawn, with `slot-hidden`) instead of going to `content`: the slot's `left`/`right` anchors now follow only the `direction`, so the hidden slot keeps the strip it would have if visible (bug #1984, fix of #1978).
- The MCP's PNG (`render_container` and `render_marking`) no longer draws what sits in a slot hidden by `visible`, nor its descendants (before, the icon of the hidden `backgroundContent` of a `swipe-to-dismiss` was painted); the app already skipped it (#1989).

## Alterado

Put an `id` in each slot (the parameter name of the platform's lambda is a good one). No component of the repository's libraries, examples and fixtures has two slots, so none of them changes. A component with a single slot, with or without `id`, and a `formatVersion` 1 to 3 component keep working as before.
