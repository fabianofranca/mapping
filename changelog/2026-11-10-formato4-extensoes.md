# Format 4: cheap extensions — 2026-11-10 (epic `epic/formato4-extensoes`, PR #1343, MCP 0.29.0, schema v18)

## Adicionado

- Component format 4: `font.verticalAlign: "cap-middle"` centers the text by the capital letter's height (from the first line to the base of the last), in the app and the viewer, in simple text, with `maxLines` and with `runs`. `middle` does not change. In versions 1 to 3 the literal is an error.
- Component format 4: number `$token` in `shadow.x`, `shadow.y` and `shadow.blur` and in each item of the `cornerRadius` list, and `stroke.dash` by expression (`$switch`/`$if`/`$field`) with number `$token` in the items; an invalid resolved value gives the warning `valor inválido em shadow.blur` or `valor inválido em stroke.dash` and counts as 0 or no dash.
- Component format 4: `layout.justify` gains `space-around` and `space-evenly`, and `layout.reverse` inverts the order of the children on the main axis (the `start` becomes the end), in frame, slot and screen arrangement.
- Component format 4: `alpha` in the color `$token` (`{ "$token": "scrim", "alpha": 0.32 }`) multiplies the color's alpha; `alpha: 0` is the transparent stop of a gradient.
- Component format 4: `stroke.offset` (px ≥ 0, literal or `$token`) moves the stroke away from the shape, in `frame`, `rect` and `ellipse`; the app, the viewer and the MCP PNG draw the ring offset from the shape (radius + `offset`), and the root's enlarged clip accounts for it.

## Alterado

- The MCP PNG (`render_marking`, `render_container` and `get_marking_image` of a container without a file) centers `font.verticalAlign: "cap-middle"` by the font's capital height (`sCapHeight`), like the app, in simple text, with several lines and with `runs`; `middle` does not change. Without a font (bars), `cap-middle` no longer falls to the top.
- The Material 3 (Compose) library goes up to version 5 (additive: swaps the drawing, without changing fields or migration) and drops the stopgaps: the shadow, the per-corner radii (the filled `text-field` and `dropdown-menu`, the `slider`, the `bottom-sheet` and the `navigation-drawer`) and the `y`/`blur` read tokens; the `carousel` scrim is a `scrim` gradient with `alpha` 0 to 0.32; the `badge` drops the fixed height; `SpaceAround`/`SpaceEvenly` and the `reverseLayout` of the layout types become `space-around`/`space-evenly` and `layout.reverse`. With `interaction: "focused"`, the `chip`, the `card` (filled and elevated), the `list-item`, the `search-bar`, the `switch`, the selected item of the `dropdown-menu` and that of the `navigation-drawer` gain the focus ring (3 in `secondary`, offset 2, outside the box).
- In the Material 3 (Compose) library, single-line labels (buttons, `extended-fab`, `chip`, `tabs`, `segmented-button`, `navigation-*`, `badge`, the action of the `snackbar` and of the `alert-dialog`, `tooltip`, `dropdown-menu`, label and value of the `text-field` and of the `secure-text-field`, `list-item`, `search-bar`, `top-app-bar`, the days and numbers of the `date-picker` and of the `time-picker`) change to `font.verticalAlign: "cap-middle"`: the center of the letter falls on the center of the box, in the app and in the MCP PNG, in any font (Compose's `Text` centers by the font's line box; with Roboto the two coincide to within 1 px). Paragraphs and multi-line text stay as they were.

## Corrigido

- In the app, `font.verticalAlign: "cap-middle"` left the text about half a unit (2 to 3 screen px at 420%) below the center, because the browser returns the font measurements rounded to the pixel at small sizes; the measurements now come from a large size, and the center matches that of the MCP PNG.
- The `validate_library` drawing test runs each case in each mode of the specialization, with the mode's tokens resolved (the size and family come from the token, not from the 14 px default), and the warning cites the mode (`…, modo dark, …`; what holds in all modes comes out once, without a mode); without `tokens`, the message does not change.
- `font.wrap: "none"` without `ellipsis` in a fixed-width box clips the line by pixel at the box (the letter that goes past comes out cut in the middle, like `white-space: nowrap; overflow: hidden`), with `align` `left`, `center` and `right`, in the app and in the MCP PNG; before, Konva removed the whole letter.
- A line of text only breaks when it exceeds the box by more than 0.5 px (a single slack, in the engine, in Konva's measurer and in the drawing): the last word no longer drops to the next line by a fraction of a pixel, and a paragraph with the width measured from the DOM breaks at the same words.
- `stroke.sides` with all four sides draws the whole outline with the corner radius, like an absent `sides` (before, they were four straight lines).
- `layout.reverse` also applies when creating an instance in a component with a slot: the first child goes to the end of the axis and the following ones stay before the last (before, they followed the direct order, to the right or below the last).
- In the Material 3 (Compose) library, explicit `Top` (`LazyColumn`) and `Start` (`LazyRow`) with `reverseLayout` keep the block at the start of the axis; the `verticalArrangement`/`horizontalArrangement` field no longer has a default (empty = Compose's default, which with `reverseLayout` is `Bottom`/`End`).
- Delivery flow: the `dist/index.html` ceiling goes up 16 KiB (from 1,933,312 to 1,949,696 bytes), once, because the `layout.reverse` placement fix exceeded the budget by 163 bytes.
- A token color in hex with 5 or 7 digits (`#12345`, `#1234567`) no longer counts as a color (before, it passed validation and the `$token`'s `alpha` was silently ignored, painting the opaque color); only 3, 4, 6 and 8 digits are a color.
- The MCP server only reads the font's capital-letter measurements when the text uses `cap-middle` (before, it read them on every text of every render).
